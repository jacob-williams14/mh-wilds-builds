## Notes for Cross-Referencing
- All 4 builds share the same weapon recommendations (Artian + 4 non-Artian elemental bows)
- All builds use Counterattack Charm III as the HR50+ alternate charm
- Skill summaries include weapon skills (from Artian/non-Artian bow decorations) separately from armor skills
- Resistance totals are for the armor loadout only (no weapon contribution)
- The Guts Seregios build is the only one without Black Eclipse (uses Razor's Edge + Protective Veil + Guts instead)
- Zoh-Gore WEX and Max Evasion Comfort share the same head+chest (Numinous Crown β + Numinous Shroud β) but differ in arms/waist/legs
- Sere-Gore and Zoh-Gore WEX share the same waist+legs (Gore Coil β + Gore Greaves β) but differ in head/chest/arms
