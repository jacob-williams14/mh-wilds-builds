## Sharpness ("Wear & Tear")

- Dual Blades in Wilds do not use the classic 66.667% sharpness-loss
  modifier; each move has a dedicated Wear & Tear multiplier. Blade Dance
  and Heavenly Blade Dance have low Wear & Tear; most other DB moves are
  high.
- Build philosophy: a set should reach at least 330 hits of sharpness
  (assuming Sixfold Demon Slash into Blade Dance). Protective Polish is
  an acceptable substitute playstyle.
- Sharpness decorations are weapon-slot only in Wilds, tightening builds.

## Comfort, Meta, and Uptime

- Demon Gauge usage (Demon Flurry, Blade Dance) makes Focus and Power
  Prolonger more valuable; Power Prolonger also extends Demon Boost Mode.
- Evade Window synergizes with Adrenaline Rush and Perfect Dodging;
  Constitution enables more dodges. Stamina is the DB comfort pain point.
- Agitator and Adrenaline Rush are uptime-dependent: Agitator is dead
  weight vs. rarely-enraged targets (Guardians); Adrenaline Rush cannot
  refresh while active and drops in multiplayer when the monster focuses
  someone else.

## Postgame Systems

- Gogma focus upgrades: Element Focus is the clear winner for DB; Attack
  and Affinity Focus penalties are net DPS losses.
- TU4.1 weapon-roll meta: Gore Magala's Tyranny + Lord's Soul on the
  Eternal Cusp. Gore's Black Eclipse (+25% affinity while Frenzy cured,
  with Antivirus) is the strongest set bonus; Gogmapocalypse shines vs.
  Gogmazios phase 3.
- Artian reinforcements: endgame and Second Wind sets max affinity, so
  fill empty slots with Attack/Element Boost. Dragon Mind's Eye maxes
  element early — prioritize Attack Boost there.
- Talismans (§2.3): meta is generated talismans, one maxed skill + one
  weapon deco slot. Primary: Critical Eye Lv 3 (1 slot) or Master's
  Touch Lv 1; secondary: Mind's Eye Lv 3; tertiary: Razor Sharp Lv 3 or
  Element Attack Lv 3. Counterattack Charm III is the smithable
  fallback. Rarity 5-6 talismans are melder fodder.

## Set Families

- Endgame Sets (§4): element-matched Eternal Cusp, identical armor shell
  across all five elements. WEX/crit core.
- Mind's Eye Sets (§5): raw-leaning variants for matchups with poor hit
  zones (triggers on hit zones 44 and below; x1.3 raw at Lv 3). Per
  element, with per-monster comparison graphs vs. the endgame variant.
- Second Wind Sets (§6): Maximum Might via G. Fulgur's bonus stamina
  bar; requires ~70% MM uptime and the "corrupted combo" (Archdemon Mode
  loop that spends no main-bar stamina). High skill ceiling, highest
  ceiling damage.
- Specializing for the Hunt (§3): dedicated Omega Planetes and Gogmazios
  sets (two-phase weapon swap for Gogmazios: Fire for phases 1-2, Dragon
  for phase 3).

## Corrupted Mantle (§7.3)

- Still the highest damage boost for DB after TU1 nerfs: 0-5% raw,
  5-10% affinity (Burst-like ramp), extra hits on basic attacks, and
  boosted Demon Gauge gains; 90s duration.
- Evasion Mantle is worth considering for longer fights.
- Mantles can be toggled without sheathing.

## Element Matchups (§4 prose)

- Fire covers the most monsters (Lala Barina, Congalala, Doshaguma,
  Nerscylla, Hirabami, G. Doshaguma, Jin Dahaad, Gypceros, Blagonga,
  Gore Magala, Lagiacrus).
- Water: G. Ebony Odogaron, Ajarakan, Nu Udra, Quematrice, Rompopolo,
  G. Fulgur Anjanath, Gravios (easiest matchup).
- Thunder: Chatacabra, Balahara, Uth Duna, Mizutsune, Seregios.
- Ice: Rey Dau, Xu Wu, Yian Kut-Ku.
- Dragon: Arkveld, Rathian, Rathalos, G. Rathalos, Zoh Shia.
- Jin Dahaad is raw-favoured outside the head/iceplates — consider raw
  skills or Blast → Fire swap.

## Meals

Set panels consistently show Hot Azuz Cuisine; stamina-cap food and Dash
Juice are called out for the Second Wind playstyle.
