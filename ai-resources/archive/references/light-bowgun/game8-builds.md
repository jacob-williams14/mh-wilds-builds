## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (Standard) — Animilater

- Slots ③③③, 286 ATK (220 base), 5% affinity, no innate element shown
- Production: Attack +5 x3, Elemental Infusion +1 (pick element needed)
- Reinforce: Attack +5 x3, Capacity Boost +1 x2
- Weapon decos (Fire example): Focus/Blaze Jewel【3】,
  Opener/Blaze Jewel【3】, Crit Elem/Blaze Jwl【3】
- Mods: Elemental Ammo Powder + Rapid Fire Magazine
- Focus chosen as a weapon skill so it can pair with Pierce Ammo
  Magazine to speed up rapid-fire gauge recovery

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Bethorned Agony

- Slots ③③③, 286 ATK (220 base) at Element Focus; 299 (230) at Attack
  Focus with -10% affinity
- Production: Element +1, Attack +5 x3; element any except status for
  elemental builds, Paralysis for the raw Normal build
- Reinforce: Attack +5 x3, Capacity Boost +1-2 x2 — aim for EX Amends
  on the highlighted reinforcements
- Focus: Element Focus for elemental ammo capacity (Attack Focus gains
  10 raw but loses affinity and capacity)
- Rolled skills seen in builds: Set Gogmapocalypse / Fulgur Anjanath's
  Will / Gore Magala's Tyranny; Group Lord's Soul
- Two Sharpness/Ammo Boost EX + Rapid Fire Magazine reaches 10 ammo
  capacity when rapid firing

### Alternate Elemental Weapons (non-Artian)

| Element | Weapon | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Blazing Kaiah | Blaze/Crit Elem【3】+ Crit Element II + Opener |
| Water | Nifl Mist | Stream/Bandolier【3】+ Crit Element II + Opener |
| Thunder | Thundacrus Rex | Bolt/Crit Elem【3】+ Crit Element II + Opener |
| Ice | Tail Catapult | Frost/Crit Elem【3】+ Crit Element II + Bandolier |

Dragon is not considered due to very low maximum capacity. Non-Artian
bowguns are fallbacks when parts or rolls are lacking.

### Recommended Mantles

- Corrupted Mantle — best offensive option (up to +10% affinity and
  +4.5% raw once conditions are met)
- Evasion Mantle — attack buff on perfect dodges, for confident dodgers

### Best Secondary Weapons

- Dimensius (Long Sword, Artian) — multi-hit moves synergize with both
  build variations; cuts tails in solo hunts
