## Weapons (Google Doc)

### Gogma Artian — Trembling Hels (every endgame meta set)

- Forge from 3x rarity-8 Attack Infusion parts of the target element.
  Elemental mapping: Fire = Fire, Water = Water, Thunder = Paralysis parts,
  Ice = Sleep parts, Dragon = Dragon (status parts trade 1 ammo capacity
  for CC ammo access)
- Ideal reinforcement rolls: 3x Attack Boost +5, 2x Capacity Boost +1 —
  savescum: forge a junk Artian, reinforce to reveal the seed, reload if
  bad ("God Roll" panel, Figure 2.2.3A shows a Greifen with
  Attack/Capacity/Attack/Capacity/Attack)
- Gogma conversion panels in the meta builds show: Sharpness/Ammo Boost
  EX (x1-2), Attack Boost EX x2, Attack Boost III x1-2
- Tarred Device: Element Focus for pure elemental; Attack Focus for
  Hybrid/Wyvernblast/Wyvernheart sets
- Set Bonus targets: Gore Magala's Tyranny (Hybrid, Pure Elemental,
  Wyvernheart), Ebony Odogaron's Power (Wyvernblast); Group: Lord's Soul
  (Hybrid and Pure Elemental panels show no group skill)
- Artian deco setups (Figures 2.2.4A-C): Budget = Blaze/Bandolier [3] +
  Crit Element/Precise [3] + Opener/Precise [3]; Meta and Tetrad variants
  swap the third jewel
- FAQ: Gogma Artian elemental guns beat craftables by over 20% DPS

### Craftable guns per element (Doc section 2.1)

- Fire: Zoh Shia gun (best); Yian Kut-Ku gun (CC option, Para ammo)
- Water: Uth Duna gun (best); Mizutsune gun (CC option, Sleep ammo)
- Thunder: Lagiacrus gun (best and CC option)
- Ice: Ore gun (best); Jin Dahaad gun (CC option, Sleep ammo)
- Raw/Spread: Seregios gun — "Bladescale Loading is so powerful that
  Seregios gun is still the weapon of choice, even over the absurdly
  overstatted Gogartians"
- HR50 Wyvernblast: Gravios gun (Gravios Gigahowl, panel in Figure 3.4A)

## Doc Set 1: Hybrid Elemental + Wyvernblast (Figures 3.1A/3.1B, HR100+)

Meta build #1 ("ascended to the top of the meta"). Shoot elemental ammo
until an opening, then Wyvernblast for easy damage. Element gives up
Offensive Guard and Ballistics to fit Artillery 3 and Special Ammo Boost

## Doc Set 2: Pure Spread (Figures 3.2A/3.2B, HR100+)

Powerful, reliable raw ammo on the Seregios gun. Mobile hop-after-every-
shot playstyle (mimics dash dancing); Evading Reload makes ammo

## Doc Set 4: Wyvernblast (Figures 3.4A/3.4B, HR100+)

Incredibly high DPS with proper execution — trounces Spread against
Arkveld — but by far the hardest HBG playstyle: precise positioning,
aggressive rolling, perfect guards only (no passive block while
crouched), and perfect focus-strike aim. Para on the gun matters given
how immobile Wyvernblast leaves you. Recharge gauge with charged Ignition
Mode focus strikes; out of gauge, fall back to Spread/Normal/Pierce.
Evade Window 2 pairs with the Azuz Tumbler meal for effective EW5.

## Notes for Cross-Referencing (Google Doc)

- Every HR100+ meta set runs the Gogma Artian Trembling Hels; the two
  HR50+ progression sets use craftable guns (Gravios Gigahowl, Blazing
  Mitran).
- God-charm variants share armor with their cheap versions except Pure
  Spread (chest + waist swap) and Wyvernheart (arms + legs swap) —
  treated as talisman variants of one set each, cheap version canonical.
- Earlier progression sets (Early LR through HR41+ Gore4 Gravios and
  Seregios Spread) fall below the HR50 endgame cutoff and are recorded
  only in general-info.
- No set in the doc shows elemental resistances — every doc-sourced
  build ships with unverified placeholder resistances.
- Doc skill-priority FAQ: Agitator > WEX for HBG (low base raw); capacity
  reinforcements prioritized over attack on Artians.
