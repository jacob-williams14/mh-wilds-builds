## Weapon Identity

HBG is a slow but versatile weapon: consistent damage output while
standing its ground against monster attacks. It lacks other weapons'
big numbers but delivers high DPS with plenty of utility, solo or in
multiplayer. Major changes from previous games in Wilds.

## Bowgun Mods

Two mod slots; match them to your ammo plan:

- Elemental / Spread / Pierce / Normal Ammo Powder — flat damage boost
  to that ammo family
- Elemental Boost Magazine — used doubled on some craftable elemental
  guns (Uth Duna, Lagiacrus, Mizutsune)
- Ignition Mode Upgrade — +15% damage to Wyvernheart/Wyvernblast;
  recommended from the very first progression build onward
- Standard Mode Upgrade — used with the Seregios Spread setup

## Ignition Mode and Special Ammo

- Wyvernblast — highest DPS special ammo; locks you crouched in place,
  demands positioning, perfect guards, and focus-strike aim
- Wyvernheart — longer range and more comfortable; significantly
  stronger after TU4 buffs but still not optimal in any single matchup
- Wyvernpiercer — paired with Wyvernblast Ignition on elemental Artian
  panels
- Wyverncounter — mutually exclusive with Wyvernblast; central to the
  Wyvernheart/power-clash build
- Recharge Ignition gauge with charged Ignition Mode focus strikes (and
  Wyverncounters on the Heart build); Spread generates the most gauge

## Ammo Management

- Most ammo is purchased or crafted; restock from the tent/camp
- Recommended endgame Item Pouch, Ammo Pouch, and Radial menus are shown
  as images (Figures 7.1A-7.3A); crafting materials include Flowfern
  (Water), Fire Herb (Fire), Thunderbug Capacitor (Thunder)
- Spread is the strongest progression ammo: highest DPS and Ignition
  gauge generation; counteract recoil by hopping after every shot
- Critical Distance: the orange crosshair indicates you are in range
  for full damage; Ballistics extends it (2 points let Spread hit
  point-blank)
- Pierce: fire along the monster's longest axis; find angles for
  maximum tick count

## Playstyles

- Raw (Spread/Pierce/Normal) vs Elemental are the two gameplay loops
  (doc Chapter 1 §6). Elemental is the endgame default; expect hunts up
  to 1.5x longer if you bring Spread everywhere (FAQ)
- Wyvernblast hybrid (elemental ammo + blast in openings) is the TU4.5
  meta top pick
- Guarding: HBG auto-blocks; manually block for Perfect Guards which
  negate stamina use (and trigger Offensive Guard on builds that run it)

## Skill Priorities (Doc 1.1)

Elemental weapon skills: Element Attack 3 (mandatory), Critical Element
3, Opening Shot 1-3, Tetrad Shot 1, Ballistics 1-3, Offensive Guard 3,
Attack Boost. Elemental armor skills: Coalescence 3, Maximum Might 3,
Burst 1, Agitator 5, Antivirus 3, Peak Performance 5.

Raw weapon skills: Critical Boost 1-5, Spread/Pierce/Normal Shots (beats
Critical Boost below ~64.5% average affinity), Ballistics 1-3 (2
mandatory for Spread), Opening Shot 3, Attack Boost. Raw armor skills:
Agitator 5, Maximum Might 3 (not for Pierce — stamina), Burst 1, Latent
Power / WEX, Flayer 1, Antivirus 3, Counterstrike 3.

Wyvernblast skills: Artillery 3 (+30% explosive), Special Ammo Boost 2
(+20% special ammo), Attack Boost, Focus 3 (+15% gauge regen); armor:
Agitator 5, Burst 1, Peak Performance 5, Counterstrike 3.

FAQ note: Agitator is prioritized over WEX on HBG because gun base raw
is low and Critical Element is a weaker crit multiplier than raw ammo's.

## Talismans

RNG talismans upgrade over craftables only if they add a useful weapon
skill for free (e.g. Tetrad Shot 1 + Agitator 1 + lvl 3 slot). Points of
Agitator are typically interchangeable with Burst, occasionally Maximum
Might or Coalescence.

## Build Assumptions and Meals

- All endgame builds assume the village food buff (+5 raw), Powercharm,
  Mega Demondrug, Might Seed, Demon Powder, and Corrupted Mantle when
  available
- Azuz Meal (Tumbler Hi) is worth 3 points of Evade Window — assumed by
  the Pure Spread and Wyvernblast sets

## Progression Summary (Chapter 2, sub-HR50 — below endgame cutoff)

- Weapon path: Hope → Chatacabra (Normal), Ore → Congalala → Mizutsune
  (Pierce), Congalala → Uth Duna → Gravios (Spread + Special),
  Seregios at ~HR34+ (Pure Spread)
- HR21+: Gravios gun Burst Boost set; HR36ish Seregios Pure Spread with
  Evade Window 5; HR41+: 4pc Gore + Gravios (Wyvernblast attack
  stacking) or upgraded Seregios Spread
- HR50+: OdoGuts Gravios and GoreGuts Element (transcribed in
  google-doc-builds.md as Doc Sets 9 and 10)
