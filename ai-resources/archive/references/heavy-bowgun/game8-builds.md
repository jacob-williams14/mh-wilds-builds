## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Greifen

- **Greifen** — Rarity 8, ATK 330 (220), Affinity 5%, Slots ③③③
- Production Bonus: Element Infusion +1, Attack +5 ×3.
  Element Type: Fire, Water, Thunder, or Ice
- Reinforcements: Capacity Boost +1 ×2, Attack +5 ×3
- Weapon Decorations (fire example — swap Blaze for the element you need):
  Opener/Blaze Jewel【3】, Bandolier/Blaze Jwl【3】, Crit Elem/Blaze Jwl【3】
- Mods: Elemental Ammo Powder + Ignition Mode Upgrade
- Used by: 4x Gore-Zoh AGI Elemental, Comfy Elemental

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Trembling Hels

- **Trembling Hels** — Rarity 8, ATK 330 (220), Affinity 0%, Slots ③③③
- Focus choice on conversion (cannot be rerolled): **Element Focus** keeps
  8-capacity elemental ammo and neutral affinity; **Attack Focus** trades
  -10% affinity and capacity for +10 raw
- Set/Group Skill targets per build: Gogmapocalypse + Lord's Soul
  (Gogmapocalypse Guts), Fulgur Anjanath's Will + Lord's Soul (Crit
  Elemental Gog), Gore Magala's Tyranny + Lord's Soul (Gore Pierce)
- Reinforcement priority: Attack +5 ×3, Capacity Boost +1-2 (EX) ×2 —
  aim for EX Amends on the capacity lines; two Sharpness/Ammo Boost EX can
  push elemental ammo capacity to 10
- Gore Pierce variant crafts with Paralysis parts (Element Type: Paralysis)
- Used by: all three HR100+ builds

### Best Non-Artian Elemental Heavy Bowguns

Listed as alternates under the 4x Gore-Zoh AGI Elemental build:

- **Blazing Mitran** (Fire) — decos: Blaze/Crit Elem Jwl【3】,
  Crit Element Jewel II【2】, Opener Jewel【1】
- **Thunderblitz Cannon** (Thunder) — decos: Bolt/Bandolier Jwl【3】,
  Crit Element Jewel II【2】, Opener Jewel【1】
- **Dalgap-of-the-Waves** (Water) — decos: Stream/Crit Elem Jwl【3】,
  Crit Element Jewel II【2】, Opener Jewel【1】
- **Steel Assault** (Ice) — decos: Frost/Crit Elem Jwl【3】,
  Crit Element Jewel II【2】, Opener Jewel【1】

### Best Non-Artian Raw Heavy Bowguns

- **Insurrection Cannon** (Seregios) — ATK 300 (200), 20% affinity, ③②①,
  innate Bladescale Loading 1 + Evading Reload 1. Spread Ammo Magazine +
  Standard Mode Upgrade. The Spread weapon of choice
- **Mountainous Roar** (Mizutsune) — ATK 315 (210), 15% affinity, ③②①,
  innate Critical Element 1 + Slicked Blade 3. Pierce Lv 3 with 6-shot
  clip; Pierce Ammo Magazine + Ignition Mod
- **Ableben Abzug** (Gore Magala) — ATK 300 (200), 20% affinity, ③②①,
  innate Critical Element 3. Pierce alternative to Mountainous Roar

### Secondary Weapons (from Max Frenzied Agitated details)

- **Varianza** (Great Sword) — Sleep GS enables bombing; shares the
  AGI/WEX/MM skill core
- **Rathling Phoenix** (Light Bowgun) — Rapid Fire Pierce 2 / Flaming
  Ammo / Poison

### Recommended Mantle

- **Corrupted Mantle** — up to +10% affinity and +4.5% attack once enough
  attacks remove the health penalty (recommended on 4x Gore-Zoh and Max
  Frenzied builds)
