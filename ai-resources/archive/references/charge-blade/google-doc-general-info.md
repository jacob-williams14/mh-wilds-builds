## Skill Priority (§2.1)

Offensive armor skills, tier 1 (best): 2/4pc Gogmazios ("20%+2 ele /
30%+4 ele is too big, too good to ignore considering Charge Blade is
officially an elemental weapon"), 4pc Gore + Antivirus 3, Agitator 5,
Burst 1 (+8/6 raw/ele, very efficient single point), Coalescence
(10-30% element after curing a status), Maximum Might 3 ("the most
efficient source of affinity"). Tier 2: Weakness Exploit (less efficient
than MM), Burst 2-5, Convert Element 3 (Dragon SAED only).

Weapon skills, tier 1: Element Attack 3 (mandatory for element builds),
sharpness skills (Savage Axe chews sharpness; 70-100 hits recommended),
Load Shells 2 (overcharge phials with red — comfort and damage). Tier 2:
Critical Boost 3-5 (strong for raw), Rapid Morph 3 (1.2x raw on morph
attacks, faster AEDF loop). Tier 3: Focus 3 (SAED only — speeds Sword
Double Slash, fewer hits for red).

## Food Skills (§2.2)

- First slot: meat base grants +2 raw.
- Second slot: Caprice Meal (Hi) (Airy Egg) +15 raw in random 10s
  intervals; Black Belt (Hi) (Droolshroom) reduces roll/hop stamina —
  helps maintain Maximum Might; Tumbler Meal adds i-frames; Moxie Meal
  (Specialty/Sild Garlic) prevents one cart.
- "There is no stand-out absolute banger meal" — eat what is available.

## RNG (Appraised) Charms (§2.3)

Mostly not worth it: "the odds of rolling perfect Charm is 1/192000" and
craftables usually win. Notably good rolls: Load Shells 2 (frees a
weapon slot), Artillery 3 (Impact SAED only), Attack Boost 3 (raw Savage
Axe only), Rapid Morph 3 (universal with AEDF loop), Coalescence 2 +
Maximum Might 2, and 1-point Agitator/Burst/WEX/Flayer with a free
level-3 slot. The guide still mainly uses craftables.

## Playstyle Notes (Part 1 highlights + §3-4)

- Savage Axe vs SAED is the core playstyle split; elemental Savage Axe
  beats raw in all matchups except Arkveld (§3.4.1); SAED got buffed
  ("MAKE SAED GREAT AGAIN!!! ... wait it's kinda good now tho").
- Perfect Guard is a subset of Guard Point since TU2 (§6).
- Load Shells should always be used post-balance-changes — comfort
  (overcharged phials extend Shield Charge) and damage on AED/AEDF
  phials (FAQ).
- Sword/Shield/Power Axe charges last 90/120/120s; each overcharged
  phial adds 12s to shield, max 180s (FAQ).
- Savage Axe refreshes easily, so Power Prolonger is skipped; Load
  Shells extends Shield Charge instead (FAQ).
- Flayer 1 is good post-TU4 buff (makes wounds appear faster) (FAQ).
- Mantles are omitted from doc builds: "they don't fundamentally change
  your approach" and reset after every quest (FAQ).
- Raw Savage Axe prefers the ED loop; elemental Savage Axe prefers the
  AED loop (§6, 9* update).
