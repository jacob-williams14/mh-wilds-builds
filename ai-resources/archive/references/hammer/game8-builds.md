## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Moteurvankel

- Artian Hammer, Rarity 8, ③③③ slots. Blast status recommended
  (Paralysis also great for immobilizing monsters).
- Production bonus (typical): Attack +5, Affinity +5, Affinity +5,
  Elemental Infusion +30. The Rey-Fulgur and Comfort Evasion variants
  instead use 3x Attack +5 production rolls.
- Reinforcements: Attack +5 x3, Affinity +5, Sharpness +30.
- Weapon decorations vary per build: Critical Jewel III【3】is constant;
  pair with Mastery Jewel【3】, Focus/Handicraft Jewel【3】, Charge
  Up/Attack Jwl【3】, or Rzr Sharp/Handicraft Jwl【3】.
- Swap Rzr Sharp/Handicraft for Grinder/Attack to reach Attack Boost 5
  if sharpness is otherwise covered.

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Bound Admonition

- Gogma Artian Hammer, Rarity 8, 1196 (230) raw, ③③③ slots, 0%
  affinity. Element: Paralysis (Blast also fine); "Any" for the
  Gogma II build.
- Focus: Attack Focus (imposes -15% affinity — amend reinforcements
  toward Affinity to counteract).
- Set-skill roll per build: Fulgur Anjanath's Will (Dosha Gogma),
  Gogmapocalypse (Gogma II), Leviathan's Fury (Gogma-Lagi). Group
  skill: Lord's Soul.
- Reinforcement prioritization: Attack +5 x3, Affinity +10 (EX),
  Sharpness +30. EX grade is a high priority for the Affinity boost.
- Weapon decorations: Critical Jewel III【3】, Mastery Jewel【3】, and
  Charge Up/Attack Jwl【3】(Charge Up/Handicraft on Gogma II).

### Best Non-Artian Hammers

| Weapon | Stats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blazing Uria | 220 raw, 250 Dragon, ③②①, 5% aff | Whiteflame Torrent 1 |
| Binder Mace | 220 raw, 250 Para, ③③①, 0% aff | Stamina Thief 3 for exhaust |

Both are Rarity 8; Blazing Uria is from the Zoh Shia tree and keeps
White Sharpness.

### Secondary Weapons

- Rathling Phoenix (Light Bowgun) — synergizes with affinity focus via
  Critical Boost III; high Pierce plus Fire coverage.
- Rathalos Firesword (Great Sword) — Critical Boost III synergy; cuts
  tails when farming specific parts.

### Recommended Mantles

- Corrupted Mantle — top damage pick: ~10% more affinity and +4.5% raw.
- Evasion Mantle — adds Evade Window; pairs well with Charging Step.

### Best Hammer Skills (page guidance)

- Damage: Weakness Exploit ★★★ (Hammer lives on the head, a prime weak
  spot), Agitator ★★★, Critical Boost ★★★, Antivirus ★★ (with 2pc Gore
  for Black Eclipse), Maximum Might ★★ (Overhead Smash combo costs no
  stamina; pair with Second Wind), Partbreaker ★★, Critical Status ★★.
- Rated low: Slugger ★ (only 1-2 extra topples at best — invest in
  damage instead), Charge Master ★, Flayer ★ (head focus limits wound
  spread).
- Comfort: Second Wind ★★★ (secondary stamina gauge; keeps Maximum
  Might active), Focus ★★★ (faster Mighty Charge), Constitution ★★★
  (for Charging Sway), Marathon Runner ★★, Evade Window/Extender ★★,
  Stamina Surge ★, Flinch Free ★ (multiplayer), Stamina Thief ★.
