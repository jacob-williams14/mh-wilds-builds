## Shared Weapon Recommendations (Game8)

### Artian Gunlance (standard): Argenesis

- Rarity 8, ATK 529 (230 display), Affinity 5%, slots ③③③
- Shelling: depends on element — Long for Water/Thunder/Dragon;
  power Slightly Strong. Long shelling buffs Wyrmstake Full Blast.
- Production bonus: Element +30, Attack +5 ×3 (element type: any)
- Reinforcements: Attack +5 ×5
- Decorations: Magazine/Attack Jwl【3】, Artillery Jewel III【3】,
  Guardian/Ironwall Jwl【3】 (or Guardian/Handicraft, or Attack Jewel III
  on the Lagi-Rey build)

### Artian Gunlance (Gogma): Auguring Omen

- Rarity 8, ATK 552 (240), Affinity -10% (Attack Focus), slots ③③③
- Attack Focus → Normal shelling, Slightly Strong. Element Focus →
  Wide shelling (used on Odo-Arkveld Wide Burst, ATK 529, 0% affinity)
- Converted from Argenesis; Attack Focus costs 15% affinity but the raw
  sources compensate
- Roll targets: Set Skill Ebony Odogaron's Power (Max Burst / Odo-Arkveld)
  or Zoh Shia's Pulse (Gogma Burst Boost / Peak Guarding); Group Skill
  Lord's Soul on all four
- Reinforcements: Attack +5 ×5; bonuses go up after Amendment
- Decorations vary by build: Artillery Jewel III【3】 +
  Guardian/Ironwall + Magazine/Ironwall (Max Burst);
  Guardian/Handicraft + Magazine/Attack (Gogma Burst Boost);
  Magazine/Ironwall + Guardian/Ironwall + Shield/Ironwall (Peak
  Guarding); Focus Jewel III + Guardian/Ironwall + Magazine/Ironwall
  (Odo-Arkveld Wide)

### Best Non-Artian Gunlances

- G. Lawful Bors — Dragon 250, ATK 552 (240), -10% affinity, ③③①,
  Guard Lv 2, Wide / Slightly Strong. Best craftable; recommended on
  all six HR50++ builds.
- Riotous Gunlance — no element (raw), ATK 506 (220), 25% affinity,
  ③②①, Bladescale Honing Lv 1, Normal / Slightly Strong. Seregios;
  pairs with the SeReyGios evade build.

### Best Secondary Weapons (from Rey Dau Gamma-Zoh Shia section)

- Ableben Abzug (Heavy Bowgun) — strong Pierce Ammo, Slicing Ammo for
  tails, rare Dragon Ammo
- Rathling Phoenix (Light Bowgun) — Rapid Fire Lv 2 Pierce / Flaming,
  Rapid Fire Poison

### Best Mantles

- Corrupted Mantle (Rey Dau Gamma-Zoh Shia section) — lowers HP but
  raises affinity and weapon stats; deal damage fast to stop the drain

### Best Skills (summary page)

- Damage: Artillery ★★★, Agitator ★★★, Burst ★★★, Load Shells ★★★
  (must-have for Wide), Offensive Guard ★★, Partbreaker ★★,

## Notes for Cross-Referencing (Game8)

- All four HR100++ builds run the Gogma Artian Auguring Omen; all six
  HR50++ builds recommend G. Lawful Bors first (Lagi-Rey prefers the
  Argenesis Artian).
- Charm pattern: most builds list an Appraised Charm (HR100+) with a
  craftable alternate — record as dual charms where both exist.
- Guts (Tenacity) appears on 9 of 10 builds (not Comfy Healing);
  meal advice pairing Moxie with Guts applies broadly.
