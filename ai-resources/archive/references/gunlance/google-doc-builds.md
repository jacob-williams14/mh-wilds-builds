## Meta Builds (all TU4 / Gogma Artian, HR100+)

The doc organizes meta builds by which set/group bonuses are rolled on the
Gogma Artian (Auguring Omen). Ideal-charm variations use an Appraised
talisman; craftable-charm variations noted where the doc provides one.

Weapon decos note (referenced by every table below): all Auguring Omen
cards run Artillery Jewel III【3】 + Guardian/Ironwall Jwl【3】 +
Magazine/Attack Jwl【3】. Exception: the HR 51 progression Argenesis
card runs Magazine/Ironwall Jwl【3】 instead of Magazine/Attack.

## Notes for Cross-Referencing (Google Doc)

- Doc "Odo 4" armor matches Game8 "Max Burst Boost Dahaad Guts" on
  4 of 5 pieces exactly (helm is G. Ebony β vs α grade) → merge.
- Doc HR 51 "Zoh-Rey-Duna" differs from Game8 "Zoh-Duna Gamma Agitator
  Burst" by one piece (waist Rey Sandcoil γ vs Duna Wildcoil γ) →
  merge; Game8 loadout (with res totals) is primary.
- Doc "Gog 4" and "Jin 4" have no Game8 counterpart (≥3 piece
  difference from every Game8 build) → doc-only builds.
- Alternative Builds 1-3 are budget reroll variants of the meta trio;
  not carried into the module to keep the build count focused.
