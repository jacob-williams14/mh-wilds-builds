## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Angelbein

- **Angelbein** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, any element, Power or
  Close-range coatings.
- Production bonus: Element +20, then Attack +5 x3 (element infusion +
  3 attack parts of the same element).
- Reinforcements: Attack +5 x5 for max raw, or Affinity +5 x2 then
  Attack +5 x3 ("at least 2 Affinity rolls" per the Sere-Gore section).
  Max Evasion variant targets 3x Affinity +5 and 2x Element +30.
- Weapon Decorations: Any Element/Bandolier Jewel【3】,
  Critical Jewel III【3】, Spread Jewel【3】(Spread vs Pierce is
  playstyle preference).

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Calamitous Angel

- **Calamitous Angel** — Rarity 8, ATK 276 (230), Slots ③③③,
  Element 260, Affinity 0%.
- Focus: Element Focus (Pierce Coating) or Attack Focus (Close-Range
  Coating). Set Skill: Gore Magala's Tyranny. Group Skill: Lord's Soul.
- Reinforcement prioritization: Element +20, Attack +5 x3 (production),
  then Attack +5 x5 — "aim for as much Attack Boosts as possible on all
  available slots".
- Weapon Decorations (Gore Lord's Soul): Critical Jewel III【3】,
  Blaze/Bandolier Jwl【3】, Spread Jewel【3】(element example — swap to
  the appropriate element). Gogma-Gore uses Critical Jewel III【3】,
  Bandolier/Precise Jwl【3】, Any Element/Crit Elem Jewel【3】.

### Best Non-Artian Elemental Bows

| Element | Bow | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Dark Filament | Rathalos · Power · Critical Boost Lv. 3 |
| Water | Heaven's Manna | Mizutsune · Pierce/Exhaust · Crit Element 1 |
| Thunder | High Voltsender | Lagiacrus · Close-range · Tetrad 2, CTR 1 |
| Ice | Singha Sharanga | Blangonga · Close-range/Poison · Tetrad Shot 3 |
| Dragon | Verzweiflung | Gore Magala · Power/Blast · Critical Element 3 |

CTR = Convert Thunder Resistance.

Notes: Heaven's Manna replaced the previous water pick — "With the
release of Mizutsune, we are now provided with another bow with a
non-negative affinity!" Verzweiflung "is still the best choice for
Dragon Bow builds" over the Zoh Shia bow.

### Best Mantles

- Evasion Mantle — longer invulnerability window for Perfect Dodges and
  an Attack Boost after a successful perfect evade.
