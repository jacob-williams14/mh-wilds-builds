## Notes for Cross-Referencing
- Builds 1–4 use Counterattack Charm III as the HR50+ alternate charm; Builds 5–6 use Exploiter/Challenger charms
- Build 6 (Gore Lord's Soul) is HR 100+ only — requires an Appraised Charm
- Skill summaries include weapon skills (from Artian/non-Artian bow decorations) separately from armor skills
- Resistance totals are for the armor loadout only (no weapon contribution)
- Build 3 (Guts Seregios) is the only build without Black Eclipse (uses Razor's Edge + Protective Veil + Guts instead)
- Build 5 (Gogma-Gore) and Build 6 (Gore Lord's Soul) both use Guts (Tenacity); Build 5 also has Mutual Hostility I
- Build 6 achieves Black Eclipse II by combining Udra head+chest with Gore arms/waist/legs
