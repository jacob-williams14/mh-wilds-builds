## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Gogma Artian Bow — Calamitous Angel

- TU4's Gogma Artian weapons "are currently the best in slot for all
  matchups". The Artian Focus chosen at upgrade determines the coating.
- Ideal reinforcements (image): 2x Attack EX (+12), 1x Attack III (+9),
  2x Ele EX (+12). "Affinity roll is by far the weakest of three so we
  tend to ignore it. 3 raw and 2 ele reinforcements assure that if you
  get a 4 EX roll, you are set with a perfect (statwise) weapon."
- Set/group bonus target depends on the build (highlighted per build).
  Gogma Artian bows of one element share a common seed — save-scum with
  Tarred Device Affinity Focus rolls to walk the seed forward.
- Anti-Arkveld raw variants shown in the build panels run 2x Attack EX,
  1x Attack III, 2x Affinity EX (+10%) instead of the Ele EX rolls.

### Ideal Standard Artian Bow — Angelbein

- Pure attack investment: 3x Attack Infusion (+5 each) plus Element
  Infusion +20 (production), then 5x Attack Boost +5 (reinforcement).
- "Any deviation on reinforcements is weaker but perfectly acceptable
  since the difference is extremely marginal." Odds chart credited to
  @via.kreatv1 (image, not transcribed).
- "If you don't care enough to min-max, just craft and use the
  G. Doshaguma bow."
