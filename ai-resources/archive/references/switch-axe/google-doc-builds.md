## Shared Weapon Recommendations (Google Doc)

### Artian Switch Axe (doc's meta weapon)

- Parts: 2x Blade + 1x Device, Rarity 8 only. Same element/status on
  2+ parts sets the weapon's element and phial type; 3x the same
  boosts the value. 3x Paralysis is the doc's optimal pick
  (Power Phial).
- Base at Rarity 8: 190 raw, 5% affinity, 20 units white sharpness,
  3-3-3 slots.
- Reinforce (5 rolls @ 10 Oricalcite each; dismantling refunds ore):
  target a total of +10-15% Affinity (2-3 rolls incl. part infusions),
  +60 Sharpness (2 rolls), rest Attack — ending near 205-210 attack,
  15-20% affinity, 80 units white sharpness, Power Phial, Paralysis.
- Weapon decorations for every set: 1x Crit Boost 3 + 1x Crit Boost 2
  (or 2x Crit Boost 3) and 1x Power Prolonger 3 (Handicraft secondary
  roll preferred).

### Skill priority (doc)

- Armor (all co-equal): Agitator 5, Weakness Exploit, Maximum Might 3,
  2pc Gore + Antivirus 3, Counterstrike 3, Burst 1.
- Weapon: Critical Boost 5 > Power Prolonger 3 > Handicraft (only as
  PP3 secondary). Rapid Morph is "fun, alternative" only; Focus is
  rated -9999 ("really, really bad").

## Build A: 4P Gore (★ top contender)

### Description (Build A)

One of the two top contenders. Full Release Slash gameplan —
Counterstrike procs off FRS hyperarmor and counters (45 s at Lv 3).
Does not hit 100% affinity but average damage matches the Agi/Max
Might set, and the 4pc Gore bonus (Black Eclipse II) plus Antivirus
feed affinity through self-frenzy. Comes with a free 2-slot and
1-slot for comfort (or Ambush 1); strong with Frenzy Mantle.
Agi4 and Wex4 variations exist (armor-set-search links in doc).

## Build C: Comfy MP (alternative build)

### Description (Build C)

Non-Agitator comfort variation, primarily for multiplayer hunts —
particularly strong vs Guardians (their special topple ends Rage Mode,
hurting Agitator uptime). The comfort factor is Decimator I
(G. Arkveld's Vitality 2pc): recover 25 HP whenever you pop a wound.
Flayer 2 is bonus wound generation. Five unused 1-slots for comfy
skills. From full stamina you can roll about twice and still have
Maximum Might active for the Full Release Slash.
