## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Dimensius

- **Dimensius** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③. Stats vary per build with the
  reinforcement mix: ATK 726 (220) at 3x Attack +5 with 2x Sharpness +30,
  up to ATK 743 (225) at 4x Attack +5 with 1x Sharpness +30 (Max Critical).
  Affinity 5–10% (1x Affinity +5 reinforcement on some builds).
- Production Bonus: Element Infusion (+20 or +30 shown) plus Attack Infusion
  +5 x3. Element Type: any of the five elements — match to the monster;
  Paralysis is the recommended general-purpose choice.
- Weapon Decorations: Critical Jewel III【3】, Critical Jewel II【2】, plus
  Mastery Jewel【3】 (Master's Touch) or Rzr Sharp/Handicraft Jwl【3】
  depending on the build. Evasive Comfy uses Expert Jewel III【3】 instead of
  Critical Jewel II.

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Headsman's Hamus

- **Headsman's Hamus** — Rarity 8, ATK 759 (230), Element 290, Affinity 0%,
  Slots ③③③. Used by both HR100++ TU4 builds (Fulgur-Lagi Guts and
  Gogma-Arkveld).
- Focus: **Attack Focus** — costs −15% affinity; Game8 recommends recovering
  10% via the Affinity Boost EX reinforcement.
- Reinforcement Prioritization: Attack +5 x3, **Affinity +10 (EX)** — EX
  grade is the high priority — and Sharpness +30.
- Rolled skills target: Set Skill **Leviathan's Fury**, Group Skill
  **Lord's Soul**. (The Google Doc instead targets Gore Magala's Tyranny —
  see the cross-referencing notes.)
- Spelling drift: this page now spells the weapon "Headsman's Hamus"; the
  Stage A fetch (2026-07-06) had "Headman's Hamus". The in-game builder
  images in the Google Doc also show "Headsman's".

### Non-Artian Alternatives (per build)

- **Wyvern Blade "Maple"** — Rarity 8, ATK 660 (200), Element 350, 10%,
  Slots ③②①, Critical Boost Lv. 3 innate. Recommended for Max Critical and
  Agitator-Guts. Decos: Draw Jewel【1】, Critical Jewel II【2】,
  Mastery Jewel【3】.
- **Khanga Rebellion** — Rarity 8, ATK 693 (210), no element, 25% affinity,
  Slots ③②①, Bladescale Honing Lv. 1. Recommended for Seregios-Gore
  (armor 70% + weapon 25% = 95% max affinity). Decos: Critical Jewel III【3】,
  Critical Jewel II【2】, Handicraft Jewel【1】.
- **Lala Ornithocton** — Rarity 8, ATK 660 (200), Paralysis 250, 15%,
  Slots ③②①, Critical Draw Lv. 2 + Critical Status Lv. 1. Recommended for
  Evasive Comfy; Blazing Rafel (Zoh Shia) is the higher-raw swap with
  Whiteflame Torrent.

### Best Mantles

- **Evasion Mantle** — instant access to Spirit Blade III; quick Spirit
  Gauge leveling.
- **Corrupted Mantle** — +4.5% attack and +10% affinity; "the mantle for
  Long Sword masters" (Iai Slash i-frames offset the health drawback).

### Best Secondary Weapons

- Rathling Phoenix (Light Bowgun) and Varianza (Great Sword) — Max Critical
  section.
- Tiltkreise (Dual Blades) and Angelbein (Bow) — Agitator-Guts section;
  both trigger Adrenaline Rush through dodge-heavy playstyles.

### Playstyle Notes

- Spam Crimson Slash Combo Loops in openings — they consume no stamina, so
  Maximum Might (and Burst) stays active through the combo.
- Iai Spirit Slash dodges consume no stamina, extending Maximum Might.
- Red Gauge lasts 50 seconds; Latent Power lasts 2 minutes (2:30 with
  Thunderous Roar I) — reach and hold Red Gauge once it activates.
