## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Omiltika

- **Omiltika** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, ATK 924–966 (220–230), Affinity 5%
- Production bonus: 3x Attack infusion +5, 1x Elemental Attack +30
- Reinforcements: 5x Attack +5 (all-Attack)
- Element type: any element for damage (Fire shown as representative);
  Paralysis recommended for a status horn (Divine Protection + All Ailments
  Negated songs)
- Elemental songlist: Self-Improvement, Elem Attack Boost, Blight Negated,
  Sonic Waves, Restore Sharpness, Echo Wave, Resounding Melody
- Status songlist: Self-Improvement, Status Attack Up, Divine Protection,
  All Ailments Negated, Echo Wave, Offset Melody
- Typical decorations: Sonorous/Attack or Sonorous/KO Jwl【3】,
  Rzr Sharp/Handicraft Jwl【3】, Element/Handicraft【3】 (or Paralyzer Jwl
  III【3】 for status)

### Gogma Artian Weapon — Onyx Choros

- **Onyx Choros** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③; elemental variant ATK 966 (230) /
  380 element; status variant ATK 861 (205) / 230 status
- Focus: Attack Focus (element builds compensate the -15% affinity with skills)
  or Element Focus (status variant)
- Reinforcements (Attack Focus): 3x Attack +5, Affinity +10 (EX),
  Sharpness +30. EX-grade Affinity is high priority to offset Attack Focus.
- Reinforcements (Element Focus, status): 2x Attack +5, Element +60 (II),
  Element +90 (EX), Sharpness +30
- Set/group skill targets: Gogmapocalypse or Ebony Odogaron's Power, with
  Lord's Soul as group skill (build-dependent; see each build)
- Typical decorations: Mind's Eye Jwl III【3】 or Attack Jewel III【3】,
  hybrid Element/Handicraft Jwl【3】, Sonorous/Attack or /Handicraft Jwl【3】

### Best Non-Artian Hunting Horns

- **Blazing Gariel** (Zoh Shia tree) — R8, 924 (220), 250 Fire, 5% aff, ③②①,
  Whiteflame Torrent 1. All-rounder raw horn: Attack and Defense Up (S),
  Stamina Use Reduced (L), Offset Melody, Health Regeneration Echo Bubble.
- **Lagia Thunderpeal** (Lagiacrus) — R8, 966 (230), 450 Thunder, ③②①,
  Slugger 2 + Convert Thunder Resistance 1. Reaches the 855 element cap with
  Thunder Res 2 + Burst 1 + Thunder Attack 3. Offset Melody.
- **Resounding Galahad** (Arkveld tree) — R8, 882 (210), 400 Dragon, ③②①,
  Horn Maestro 2 + Slugger 2. Attack Up (L), Extend All Melodies, Restore
  Sharpness, Resounding Melody. Best non-Artian pick and top secondary.
- **Queen Chordmaker** (Rathian) — R8, 840 (200), 250 Poison, 15% aff, ③②①,
  Critical Status 3. Best health-support horn: Attack Up (L), Recovery
  Speed (L), Melody of Life, Health Regeneration Echo Bubble.
- **Summoning Bell / Gramklang** — Affinity Up/Health Recovery + Earplugs (L)
  songs; Stamina Regen (Bell) or Evasion & Move Speed Up (Gramklang) bubbles.

### Secondary Weapons

- **Resounding Galahad (HH)** — All Melodies Extended lets a secondary horn
  extend the primary horn's buffs by 20s (+20 more per Encore), enabling a
  near-infinite buff loop.

### Mantle Guidance

- **Corrupted Mantle** — best damage option (~+10% affinity, +4.5% attack) at
  the cost of gradual health loss. Combo: mantle on → Echo Bubble → Echo Wave
  spam or Resounding Melody.
- **Rocksteady Mantle** — hyper armor protects performances from interruption.

## Notes for Cross-Referencing

- Rank tiers per the details-page grouping: Builds 1-4 are HR100+ (1.041/TU4/
  TU3); Builds 5-9 are HR50+ (TU1-TU2).
- Builds 5-9 carry dual charms: named charm at HR50+, Appraised Talisman at
  HR100+.
- Game8's Gogmapocalypse Guts (Build 3) armor matches the Google Doc's "Ele
  Stacking (Hi Element)" set 5/5 pieces — merge candidate.
- Game8's Gog Burst-Guts (Build 2) shares the G. Ebony + Udra + Gogmazios
  Coil core with the Google Doc's "Raw Stacking (Lo/Mid Element)" but differs
  in arms and legs (2 pieces) — kept separate.
- Playstyle guidance (details page): build Self-Improvement first, Echo
  Bubble + Echo Wave is the main DPS loop, Focus Strike: Reverb interrupts
  attacks and refreshes bubble charges, end queues with a dodge or Encore.
