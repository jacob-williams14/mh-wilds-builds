## Weapon Identity

- Wilds HH centers on **Echo Bubbles** and **Echo Waves**: set a bubble, then
  spam Echo Wave performances inside it for large chunks of elemental,
  status, or raw damage. Bubble placement got a 50% buff in TU2 and Focus
  Strike lets you place one free if timed right.
- Much of the kit (Echo Wave except slash types, Echo Bubble, Resounding
  Melody) **cannot crit**, which is why the meta stacks raw and element over
  affinity.
- HH is the only weapon that truly exploits weapon swap: "backup horning"
  pairs a main horn with a swap horn carrying different buffs (e.g. Attack
  Up (L) + Earplugs (L)) without a swap penalty. Only swap while running to
  the monster or between areas.
- One song at a time: always Encore immediately after a single Echo Wave —
  chaining waves or Performance Beats is a damage loss (unbuffed even in
  TU4).

## Song Rotation (assumed playstyle)

1. Queue **Self-Improvement** notes on approach (on foot or Seikret).
2. Open with attack/element buff songs, then utility; keep buffs refreshed
   only when timers near expiry.
3. Drop an **Echo Bubble**, then loop Echo Wave → single Encore.
4. Use **Focus Strike: Reverb** on wounds to interrupt attacks and refresh
   bubble charges; chain its 5 note inputs into more songs.
5. Keep **Offset Melody** (Special Performance) ready to counter big attacks;
   Hilt Stab (buffed in TU2.5) cancels Encore/Resounding recovery and baits
   counters.
6. End queues with a dodge or Encore to avoid getting clipped mid-recital.

## Skill Priority (endgame, raw stacking)

- S tier: Guts (AT gamma pieces, ~12 raw equivalent + cart save), Burst
  Boost, Powerhouse (Offset horns), Gogmapocalypse, Agitator 1-5, Burst 1-5,
  Counterstrike 1-3.
- A tier: Adrenaline Rush 1, Flayer 1 (wound consistency → Focus Strike
  spam), Maximum Might 1-3, Convert Element 3 (AT Arkveld chest; mandatory
  for Dragon).
- B tier: Latent Power 1-5, Weakness Exploit 1-5 (weaker than LP this meta).
- Weapon skills: Horn Maestro 2 is a mandatory "tax" (melody duration +
  bubble radius/duration); then Attack Boost 4-5; Element Attack 3
  outperforms equivalent Crit Boost on elemental horns; Crit Boost for raw
  sets only; Bludgeoner 3 only on no-sharpness Attack Focus Gogma horns.

## Talismans

- No RNG talismans required. Best flawless: Agitator III (from AT Jin
  Dahaad).
- Good RNG rolls: R5 with Agitator/Burst/Flayer 1 + a 3-slot; best-in-slot is
  R6 Attack Boost 2 + Maximum Might 1/Counterstrike with 2-1-0 slots.

## Other Considerations

- Eat for **Moxie (Hi)** whenever possible — the game consumes Moxie before
  Guts, so you keep both the raw bonus and the cart protection.
- Mantles by setup: Evasion Mantle with Adrenaline Rush sets; Rocksteady for
  performance consistency; Corrupted for raw/affinity/Latent Power uptime.
- Artian upgrade routing: craft 3 Attack + matching element components →
  Gogma upgrade with Attack Focus → roll Ebony Odogaron's Power (element) or
  Doshaguma's Might (raw/status) with Lord's Soul → reinforce 2x Attack EX,
  1-2x Attack III, Sharpness EX.
- Base element rolls have NO effect on Echo Waves — avoid element
  reinforcements on elemental horns.

## Matchup Notes

- Prioritize Fire, Water, and Dragon Artian rolls for maximum coverage.
- Gogmazios starts Fire and ends Dragon; Zoh Shia, Gogmazios, and AT Arkveld
  favor Dragon with Convert Element.
- Omega Planetes: bring G. Resounding Galahad for Sonic Barrier damage
  reduction (Thunder Artian if your squad is expert and speed-focused);
  healing horns as main are non-viable.
