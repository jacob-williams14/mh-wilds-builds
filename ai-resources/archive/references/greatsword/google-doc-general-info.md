## Gameplay Overview

Great Sword is a rather simple weapon at its core: "beeg hits, beeg damage
numbers, monster beeg dead." All its subtleties come from positioning and
timing to land chunky hits. Core loop: run around until you find an opening
for strong charged attacks that chain into even stronger attacks. Tackle and
Offset Attack power through monster attacks while hitting back — Offset
Attack is a very good damaging attack whether or not you counter with it.
(Doc: "A more detailed gameplay guide is coming soon.")

## Gogma Artian Mechanics (TU4)

- Upgrading to Gogma Artian rolls two rerollable active skills: a set skill
  and a group skill (e.g. Gore Magala's set bonus alongside Lord's Soul).
  Ideal roll: Soul of the Dark Knight (Bale's set) + Lord's Soul.
- Focus choice affects final stats and cannot be rerolled: Attack Focus
  (+10 attack, -15% affinity, +10 sharpness), Affinity Focus (+10% affinity,
  -10 attack, -2 element, -10 sharpness), Element Focus (+4 element, -5%
  affinity). Affinity Focus = most consistent; Attack Focus = biggest TCS
  hits (TCS buffed in TU4).
- New reinforcement levels run I to EX; max 2 reinforcements of the same
  level.

## Notes

- Adjust decos to reflect actual Gogma rolls and charms — "Don't just copy
  mindlessly!"
- MathHunter is a simulator, not a benchmark; buff uptimes (Agitator, Latent
  Power) and realized combos vary. The sets are close — choose by what they
  are good for.
- Some sets conditionally exceed the 100% affinity cap; unlikely to matter
  without a perfect random charm. If it bothers you, swap affinity skills for
  proc skills like Flayer and Convert Element ("both are unironically good").
