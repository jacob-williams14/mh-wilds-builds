## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Varianza

- **Varianza** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, Element: Sleep (Paralysis also fine)
- Stat lines vary slightly per build (ATK 1032–1104 / 5–20% affinity) depending
  on
  infusion mix:
  - Max-raw variant (Ful-Gore Zoh, Lagi-Guts): 3x Attack infusion, reinforce 5x
    Attack +5
  - Balanced variant (LAgiZoh, GoreDuna): 1x Affinity + 2x Attack infusion, reinforce
    5x Attack +5
  - Affinity variant (Lagi-Rey): 1x Attack + 2x Affinity infusion, reinforce 4x
    Attack +5,
    1x Affinity +5
- Weapon Decorations: Critical Jewel III【3】, Focus/Handicraft Jewel【3】,
  Guardian/Handicraft Jwl【3】 (or Critical Jewel II【2】in max-crit variants). Use
  plain
  Focus Jewel III / Guardian Jewel III if you lack the hybrid jewels.

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Ostrak Oblivion

- **Ostrak Oblivion** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, converted from Varianza
- Focus choice (cannot be rerolled): **Attack Focus** (used by Bale Dahaad
  Gore, Comfy
  Arkveld, Offense Arkveld — ATK 1104/0%) or **Affinity Focus** (used by GogMax
  Agi Wex —
  ATK 1056/15%). Game8: "When converting your Varianza to Ostrak Oblivion, you
  must select
  the Affinity Focus option \[for GogMax\]. Although you will lose 10 Raw
  Attack in doing
  this, you can easily gain it back once you amend your reinforcements."
- Reinforcement priority (Attack Focus builds): Attack +5 ×3, Affinity +10 (EX),
  Sharpness +30. (Affinity Focus build: 5x Attack +5.)
- Rolled Set Skill targets per build: Gore Magala's Tyranny + Lord's Soul (Bale
  Dahaad
  Gore), Fulgur Anjanath's Will (Comfy Arkveld), Soul of the Dark Knight (Offense
  Arkveld), Zoh Shia's Pulse + Lord's Soul (GogMax)
- Weapon Decorations: Critical Jewel III【3】, Guardian/Handicraft Jwl【3】,
  Focus/Handicraft Jewel【3】 (Bale Dahaad Gore swaps Guardian/Handicraft for
  Critical Jewel II【2】)

### Best Non-Artian Alternative

- **Blazing Veiah** (Zoh Shia) — Rarity 8, ATK 1056 (220), Dragon 300, Affinity
  5%,
  Slots ③②①, Whiteflame Torrent Lv. 1. White Sharpness, high raw. Decos:
  Critical Jewel III【3】, Focus Jewel II【2】, Dragon Jewel【1】.
- **True Shadowbringer** — Rarity 8, ATK 1056 (220), Dragon 600, 0%, Slots ③②①,
  Darkside Lv. 1 (Dark Knight build only). Decos: Focus/Dragon Jewel【3】,
  Guardian Jewel II【2】, Guardian Jewel【1】.

### Best Mantles

- **Rocksteady Mantle** — negates knockback so charge attacks aren't
  interrupted; also
  helps activate Counterstrike. Does not negate damage — watch HP.

### Best Secondary Weapons

- **Resounding Galahad** (Hunting Horn) — Attack Up (L), Knockback Negated, Restore
  Sharpness songs; good solo and multiplayer.
- **White Rathling Phoenix** (Light Bowgun) — Rapid Fire Lv 2 Spread
  (synergizes with
  Flayer/WEX), Rapid Fire Flaming ammo, plus Poison/Recover/Armor support ammo.

### Playstyle Notes (from the Critical Sleep GS section; applies to GS generally)

- Fast TCS combos are the core damage loop; use Offset Attacks whenever possible
  (charged Offset → Follow-up Cross Slash).
- Tackle through roars/attacks to keep combos going — hyper-armoring through an
  attack
  that would send you flying activates Counterstrike (+25 raw).
- Perfect Guards activate Offensive Guard (+15% raw); Power Clashes trigger
  Binding Counter where available.
- On wounds, run the TCS-Perforate Loop. On sleeping monsters, plant Large/Mega
  Barrel
  Bombs and land a wake-up True Charged Slash.

### Best Skills (Game8 star ratings)

- Damage ★★★: Weakness Exploit, Agitator, Maximum Might, Counterstrike.
  ★★: Burst, Critical Boost, Antivirus, Partbreaker, Critical Draw, Offensive Guard.
  ★: Flayer, Attack Boost, Critical Element, Elemental Attack, Latent Power.
- Comfort ★★★: Focus ("must-have for any Great Sword build").
  ★★: Constitution, Second Wind, Guard. ★: Decimator, Handicraft, Shock Absorber,
  Quick Sheathe, Speed Sharpening.
