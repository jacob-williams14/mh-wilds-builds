## Weapons

### Gogma Artian — Ostrak Oblivion (the chase weapon)

Assumed Gogma Artian setup used by every Ostrak set in the doc (image 02,
"Reinforce Weapon" panel):

- Blades/Tube: 3x Dragon Attack Infusion
- Tarred Device: Affinity Focus
- Set Bonus Skill: Soul of the Dark Knight (Bale's set)
- Group Skill: Lord's Soul
- Reinforcements: Sharpness/Ammo Boost EX, Attack Boost EX x2, Attack Boost
  III x2
- Focus options: Attack Focus (+10 atk, -15% aff, +10 sharp), Affinity Focus
  (+10% aff, -10 atk, -2 ele, -10 sharp), Element Focus (+4 ele, -5% aff).
  Affinity Focus for most consistent damage; Attack Focus for the biggest TCS
  hits (TCS buffed in TU4). Critical Draw set specifically wants Attack
  Infusion.
- Max 2 reinforcements of the same level (no 3x Attack EX).
- Weapon decos (image 01 shows the pre-conversion Varianza panel: 225 raw, 5%
  affinity, Sleep 28): Critical Jewel III [3], Critical Jewel II [2],
  Focus/Handicraft Jewel [3] — replace Focus/Handicraft with any deco giving
  Focus 3.

### Craftable — (Best) True Shadowbringer

- 220 raw, 0% affinity, Dragon 60, Darkside 1 (image 03). Decos:
  Focus/Handicraft Jewel [3], Critical Jewel II [2], Critical Jewel [1].
- Optimal weapon choice even over pre-TU4 Artians. Darkside enhances the three
  standard charged slashes at the cost of HP per Charged Slash (recoverable
  health — run Recovery Speed 3 and Wild Jerky).
- The self-drain triggers Resentment consistently (drain happens at the start
  of the Charged Slash, so every slash benefits) — GS is the only weapon class
  that can consistently use Resentment. Artians cannot.
- Stuck with Dragon element: underperforms into Dragon-resistant monsters but
  slays Gogmazios — use it to farm Gogma weapons (make Fire and Thunder Gogmas
  first).

### Craftable — High Lagia Blade

- 220 raw, 0% affinity, Thunder 55, Convert Thunder Resistance 1, Focus 2
  (image 04). Decos: Critical Jewel III [3], Critical Jewel II [2],
  Focus Jewel [1].
- Great craftable; good for hunting Omega to craft Shadowbringer. Slots
  Critical Boost 5 + Focus 3.

## Set 1: (Still Good) Bale + Lord's Soul + Bad Blood (image 05)

True Shadowbringer set. If you lack an optimal Gog Artian and can play around
the self-damage, this stays on par with an optimal Gog set (which cannot use
Darkside or Resentment). Bad Blood buffed in TU4. 2pc Bale adds a ~10%
shockwave to charged slashes and ~20% elemental damage while you have
recoverable health.

## Extra Sets (RNG talismans; by &Knuckles)

Best sets always run Lord's Soul + 2pc Bale + one extra bonus (Doshaguma
+10 raw, Ebony +8 raw, Fulgur affinity, Scorcher flat damage). All use an
Attack Boost 2 + "good skill" R8 random charm and Ostrak Oblivion. Condensed
— armor combos below; full skill lists live in images 10-17.

- Fulgur (image 11): G. Fulgur Helm β / Udra Miremail γ / G. Fulgur
  Vambraces β / Rey Sandcoil γ / Bale Sollerets α; AB2+Flayer1 charm. If you
  can't offset or must roll a lot (e.g. Omega). Image 16 variant swaps arms
  to Bale Gauntlets α (one piece; sidegrade with a point of Resentment).
- Scorcher (image 12): G. Rathalos Helm β / Dahaad Shardmail γ / Bale
  Gauntlets α / G. Rathalos Coil α / Dahaad Shardgreaves γ; AB2+WEX1 charm.
  2pc Jin bonus; +5.25% damage when Jin is active.
- Alternative Scorcher (image 13): G. Rathalos Helm β / Udra Miremail γ /
  G. Rathalos Vambraces β / Rey Sandcoil γ / Bale Sollerets α; AB2+LP1 charm.
  Best Attack Focus set; depends on Latent Power (~55% uptime break-even).
- Scorcher sidegrade (image 17): G. Rathalos Helm β / Udra Miremail γ / Bale
  Gauntlets α / Rey Sandcoil γ / Bale Sollerets α; AB2+Agi1 charm.

sets. Best found: Gore Helm β / Udra Miremail γ / Bale Gauntlets α / Rey
Sandcoil γ / Bale Sollerets α with Attack Focus (Gore Magala's Tyranny
rolled). Competitive but not exceptional, no comfort.

## Notes for Cross-Referencing

- Every doc set targets >90% affinity via WEX/MM/Agitator/Latent Power
  combos; raw is prioritized over affinity when the two conflict ("max
  agitator first").
- Core recurring pieces: Udra Miremail γ (Counterstrike 2), Rey Sandhelm γ,
  Bale Gauntlets α / Flanchard α / Sollerets α — matches Game8's TU4 builds'
  use of Udra Miremail γ.
- The doc's NOTES section: adjust decos to your actual Gogma rolls and charm;
  MathHunter numbers are a simulator, not a score — pick sets by what they
  are good for.
- No resistance or defense totals anywhere in the doc (MathHunter Defense
  panel collapsed in every screenshot) — module uses unverified placeholders
  for Google-Doc-only builds.
