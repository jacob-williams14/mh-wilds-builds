## Build 2: Fulgur-Odo Guts IG (★★★★★)

### Description
This is a Raw and Affinity-focused Insect Glaive build that uses a Gogma Artian weapon and the new Armor Transcendence system. It takes advantage of Fulgur Anjanath's Will and Ebony Odogaron's Power, along with Guts (Tenacity).

For sharpness management, we tried fitting in Master's Touch to pair with the build's high affinity. For comfort, we added Divine Blessing, the pieces themselves already come with Quick Sheathe. Feel free to swap these 1-slot decos out for any other preferred skill.

## Build 3: Gogmazios Guts IG (★★★★★)

### Description
This is an IG Gogmazios build that uses a Gogma Artian weapon. It makes full use of Mutual Hostility II, which not only gives a significant boost to your elemental damage output, but also provides a temporary barrier that nullifies damage. This results in a strong and comfortable elemental build.

This build utilizes Challenger Charm III to maximize decoration space for all other skills. But feel free to modify it according to your preference. Appraised Talismans can give you more comfort in certain aspects with their extra deco slots and skills, but know you will have to make sacrifices in skill levels to accommodate them.

## Notes for Cross-Referencing
- All 4 builds share the same Artian weapon recommendation (Limbo Ltor)
- Builds 1, 3, and 4 use Challenger Charm III as the HR50+ alternate charm
- Build 2 (Fulgur-Odo) uses Mighty Charm III as the HR50+ alternate and has a unique appraised charm with Critical Eye + Maximum Might + Counterstrike
- Builds 2, 3, and 4 all use Udra Miremail γ as the chest piece (Counterstrike Lv. 2)
- Builds 1 and 3 both use Gogmazios Vambraces α (Maximum Might Lv. 2, Agitator Lv. 1)
- All builds include Guts (Tenacity) as a group/set skill
- All builds feature Weakness Exploit 5 and high Agitator levels
- Build 2 is the only one using Armor Transcendence (+ Transcend on Head, Arms, and Waist)
- Build 3 (Gogmazios Guts) is the only elemental-focused build with Mutual Hostility II
- Builds 1 and 2 use Master's Touch via weapon decorations for sharpness management
- Build 4 uses Razor Sharp 3 and Handicraft 1 via weapon for sharpness instead
