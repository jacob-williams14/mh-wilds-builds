## Weapon Overview

Longsword has plenty of mobility, chainable counters, and quick,
cancellable moves. Wilds LS focuses on a single, short damage loop rather
than the counter-heavy style of previous games.

## Core Mechanics

- **Spirit Gauge / Spirit Levels**: normal attacks generate gauge; Spirit
  Attacks (ZR) spend it. Gauge-spending attacks have innate Flinch Free.
- Iai Slash generates 100% gauge over 15 seconds. Foresight Slash consumes
  100% gauge to counter (with no gauge it generates 10% instead).
- Spirit Levels: White → Yellow → Red, giving 2/5/10% attack. Gained from
  landing Spirit Roundslash, countering with Iai Spirit Slash, or breaking
  a wound with Focus Strike.
- Red Spirit changes moves: Foresight → Foresight Whirl Slash; Overhead /
  Crescent Slash → Crimson I/II/III; Spinning Crimson Slash unlocks after
  Roundslash.
- Red Gauge decays over 50 seconds. Regen while red: Foresight Whirl Slash
  (consumes 16%, recovers 4% per timed hit), Spirit Roundslash (halts decay
  5s first time; restores 20/10/6% the first 3 times, regen shared with
  Spinning Crimson Slash), Iai Spirit Slash (restores 20% on successful
  counter, once).

## Optimal Damage Loop

Crimson Slash I → Spirit Slash I (X → ZR), looped on Red Spirit. Every
input consumes 1 sharpness with no reduction modifiers, so Master's Touch
is the sharpness-maintenance skill of choice.

## Skill Priority (§2.1)

Tier 1 armor skills: Weakness Exploit, Maximum Might, 2pc Gore + Antivirus
3, Agitator, Quick Sheathe 3, Counterstrike, Burst 1. Tier 2: Adrenaline
Rush, Coalescence, Burst 2–5 (full element sets only).

Tier 1 weapon skills: Critical Boost 5 ("the strongest skill in the game
for raw sets"), Master's Touch. Tier 2 (element sets only): Element
Attack 3, Critical Element 3.

## Food (§2.2)

- Slot 1: Meat base (+2 raw).
- Slot 2: Caprice Meal (Hi) (Airy Egg) +15 raw in random 10s intervals;
  Black Belt (Hi) (Droolshroom) reduces Foresight Slash stamina cost
  (pairs with Constitution 3 for Maximum Might uptime); Tumbler Meal adds
  dodge i-frames; Moxie Meal (Speciality/Sild Garlic) prevents one cart.
- Slot 3 depends on set and location. "No stand-out absolute banger meal."

## FAQ Highlights (§7)

- No Focus / Power Prolonger: LS has enough tools (Spirit Charge, FSS, ISS,
  Focus Strike) to reach and hold Red Spirit.
- Quick Sheathe 3: speeds Special Sheathe → ISS, the best gauge generator.
- Flayer: doesn't work on most relevant LS attacks — but buffed in TU4,
  "so it's good now."
- Whiteflame Torrent: ~50 flat damage proc with a 3s cooldown (~every
  7–8s).
- No mantles listed because they don't reset on quest start/end.
- Antivirus gives 10% affinity (English description wrong); cured Frenzy
  gives 15%.
- Constitution: Foresight Slash costs stamina; helps Maximum Might uptime.
- RNG charms: odds of beating craftable charms are extremely low.
