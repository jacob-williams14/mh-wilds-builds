### Artian Weapon (standard) — Verdoloto

- **Verdoloto** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, Element: Any (match the monster)
- Stat lines vary per build by infusion mix:
  - Attack variant (Max Critical Rey Gore: ATK 315/5%; Lagi-Rey: ATK 301/5%):
    Attack Infusion x3, Production Bonus Element +20 + Attack +5 x3.
    Reinforce: Attack +5 x4, Sharpness +30 (Lagi-Rey swaps two Attack +5 for
    Element +30 x2).

    Production Bonus Element +20 + Affinity +5 x3. Reinforce: Element +30 x4,
    Sharpness +30.

  Jwl【3】 and an elemental attack/hybrid jewel (Mastery Jewel【3】 on the
  Self-Heal and Lagi-Odo variants). Plain Guardian III works if you lack
  hybrids. Status jewels have no hybrid counterparts.

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Kyrie Verd

- **Kyrie Verd** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, used by all four HR100++ builds
- Focus choice (cannot be rerolled): **Attack Focus** (Gog-Guts Elemental,
  Gog-Guts Wide-Range, Lagi-Odo Guts — ATK 322/0%; the -15% affinity from
  Attack Focus is offset by an Affinity +10 (EX) reinforcement) or
  **Element Focus** (Arkveld Gamma Agitator — ATK 315, Ele 320, 10%)
- Production Bonus: Element +30, Attack +5 x3. Element Type: Any
  (Lagi-Odo rolls Paralysis; Blast also fine)
- Reinforcement priority (Attack Focus): Attack +5 x3, Affinity +10 (EX),
  Sharpness +30 — EX-grade Affinity is high priority. (Element Focus build:
  Attack +5 x2, Affinity +5 x2, Sharpness +30.) Amend reinforcements for
  better stats.
- Rolled Set/Group Skill targets per build: Gogmapocalypse + Lord's Soul
  (both Gog-Guts builds), Ebony Odogaron's Power + any group skill (Arkveld
  Gamma Agitator), Ebony Odogaron's Power + Lord's Soul (Lagi-Odo Guts)
- Weapon Decorations: Critical Jewel III【3】 + Blaze/Crit Elem Jwl【3】 +
  Shield/Ironwall Jwl【3】 (Gog-Guts Elemental); Guardian/Ironwall +
  Dragon/Ironwall + Rzr Sharp/Ironwall【3】 (Arkveld Gamma); Critical III +
  Blaze/Handicraft + Shield/Ironwall【3】 (Wide-Range); Critical III +
  Guardian/Ironwall + Mastery【3】 (Lagi-Odo). Swap the elemental jewel to
  match your weapon's element.

### Best Mantles

- **Corrupted Mantle** — more damage while active (Affinity and Attack) but
  drains HP when you are not attacking. High risk, high reward.

### Best Secondary Weapons

- **Chrono Gear** (Charge Blade) — sword mode plays like SnS; Savage Axe and
  SAED do not consume stamina so Maximum Might stays active.

### Playstyle and Combos (HR 50++ section)

- Open with a Perfect Guard (activates Offensive Guard, nullifies the roar)
  or a Sneak Attack for burst damage.
- Use quick combos (Lateral Slash combo) to keep damage flowing and
  activate Burst; clear Frenzy fast for Antivirus/Coalescence.
- Reposition with Side Slash, Advancing Slash, or Sliding Swipe.
- Negate attacks with Perfect Guard; use Backstep for hits too risky to
  block.
- Weave in Shield Bashes to rack up Stun damage.
- When the monster is down or stunned, land a Perfect Rush for a big burst.

### Best Skills (Game8 star ratings)

- Damage ★★★: Weakness Exploit, Maximum Might, Element Attack, Critical
  Element. ★★: Offensive Guard, Burst. ★: Attack Boost, Critical Boost,
  Critical Eye.
- Comfort ★★★: Protective Polish, Handicraft. ★★: Razor Sharp, Master's
  Touch, Evade Extender. ★: Guard.
