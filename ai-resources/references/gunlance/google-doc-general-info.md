## Shelling Formula (build-guide assumptions)

```text
((Base Raw x Raw Multiplier) + Flat Raw Bonus) x (MV/100)
  + Fixed Fire Damage x Fire HZV
```

- Shelling cannot crit, and its raw portion ignores hitzone values —
  stack raw, not affinity or element.
- Artillery is a raw multiplier (1.05 / 1.10 / 1.15) and adds +3 fixed
  fire per level.
- Agitator, Counterstrike, etc. are flat raw bonuses.

## Skill Priority

- Armor skills: "Raw is great, the primary thing you want to build
  for. Crit is good, slightly better on Long. Earplugs is practically
  essential — it allows many free openings. Element is just an added
  bonus."
- Weapon skills, ranked: 1) Artillery 3 (never go without),
  1.5) Load Shells 2 (extra shell + faster reloads), 2) Offensive
  Guard (+5% raw per level for 12 s on well-timed guards/Guard
  Points), 2.5) Guard (Multi Wyrmstake Full Blast Guard Points are not
  Perfect Guards — low Guard gets you knocked out of the move),
  4) Attack Boost (good, rarely fits). Focus charges Wyvern's Fire and
  Charged Shelling faster but provides less value.

## Meta Shelling Types

- "Normal > Wide >> Long."
- Normal is the best general type now that Gogma Artian shelling type
  follows the Focus roll (Attack Focus → Normal). Outdamages the other
  types in most combos.
- Wide is worth keeping for matchups that favor shell hopping and
  Wyvern's Fire due to small openings — specifically Omega Planetes,
  AT Jin Dahaad, AT Arkveld.
- "Long is dead while stuck on Affinity Focus."
- Base Artian shelling by element: Wide = Blast/Paralysis/Poison;
  Normal = Sleep/Ice/Fire/Raw; Long = Dragon/Thunder/Water. Raw only
  gets level 2 shelling power.
- Gogma Focus table: Attack Focus +10 raw / -15% affinity / Normal;
  Affinity Focus -10 raw / +10% affinity / +30 element / Long;
  Element Focus -5% affinity / +80 element / Wide. Attack Focus is
  best — almost half of Normal-shelling DPS comes from shells, which
  do not crit.

## Best Gunlances (doc)

- All the best Gunlances have level 3 shelling; elemental beats
  status, raw matters most.
- "Gogma Artians even at lowest attack rolls outclass all craftables.
  Match the element to the monster's elemental weakness."
- Best per type (Normal/Wide/Long alike): Argenesis / Gogma
  (Auguring Omen).
- Artian rolls: 3 Attack infusions; 5 Attack reinforcements ideal
  (1/1024) or 4 Attack + anything — "settle for good enough (3-4
  attack rolls)". 1 Sharpness roll is defensible for Burst Fire loops.
- Gogma reinforcements: max 2 per level, not per category (e.g. 2 Atk
  EX, 2 Atk III OK). Values: Attack +6/+9/+12 (II/III/EX), Affinity
  +6/+8/+10%, Element +60/+90, Sharpness +30/+50.
- Ideal weapon decorations: Artillery 3, Load Shells/Attack,
  Offensive Guard/Ironwall. Guard Up deco can swap in for Offensive
  Guard by matchup; extra Guard levels are comfort.

## FAQs (selected)

- Element importance: not important — shells don't scale from element;
  raw stacking is prioritized.
- Normal vs Wide gap: matchup dependent; Wide is at worst ~2-3 min
  slower.
- Flayer/Scorcher/Whiteflame: no — shells cannot proc skill criticals.
- Affinity: raw first, but affinity has value via wyrmstake tick crits.

## Playstyle Notes (Part 1 summary)

Part 1 (Gameplay Guide) covers inputs, ammo types (Shells, Wyrmstake,
Wyvern's Fire), shelling types/power, Focus Mode/Strike, Perfect
Guard, Guard Points, Power Clash, and Sneak Attack; the core optimal
damage combo is the Wyrmstake Full Blast Combo with Perfect Guard,
Sneak Attack, and Power Clash follow-ups. Multi Wyrmstake Full Blast
has Guard Points that are not Perfect Guards (hence the Guard skill
priority above). Full move-by-move detail NOT TRANSCRIBED (gameplay
tab prose, not build data).
