## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Skyscraper

- **Skyscraper** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, Element: Paralysis (Blast also fine)
- Production bonus: 3x Attack infusion (Element +30, Attack +5 x3)
- Reinforcements: 4-5x Attack +5, remainder Sharpness +30 (per-build tables
  vary between 5x Attack and 4x Attack + 1x Sharpness)
- Weapon Decorations (HR50 builds): Guardian/Ironwall Jwl【3】, Critical
  Jewel III【3】, Critical Jewel II【2】. Lagi-Guts Critical swaps to
  Guardian/Handicraft Jwl【3】+ Critical Jewel III【3】+ Mastery Jewel【3】

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Aether Pike

- **Aether Pike** — Rarity 8, Slots ③③③, used by all five Gogma HR100 builds
- Focus: Attack Focus (-15% affinity, countered via Affinity reinforcement);
  Fulgur-Dahaad may also run Element Focus
- Set Skill varies per build: Gore Magala's Tyranny (AGI-WEX-Flayer,
  Fulgur-Dahaad), Arkveld's Hunger (Gogma II), Jin Dahaad's Revolt (Binding
  Counter), Fulgur Anjanath's Will (Fulgur-Lagi). Group Skill: Lord's Soul
  (or Any)
- Reinforcements: Attack +5 x3, Affinity +10 (EX), Sharpness +30 — EX-grade
  Affinity is the top amendment priority
- Weapon Decorations: Critical Jewel III【3】, Mastery Jewel【3】,
  Shield/Ironwall Jwl【3】(Fulgur-Dahaad: Critical III + Guardian/Ironwall +
  Rzr Sharp/Handicraft)

### Non-Artian Alternatives

- **Blazing Sital** — Rarity 8, ATK 506 (220), Fire 200, 5% affinity, Slots
  ③②①, Whiteflame Torrent Lv. 1. White Sharpness, high raw. Decos:
  Guardian/Ironwall Jwl【3】, Critical Jewel II【2】, Critical Jewel【1】
- **High Acrus Lance** — Rarity 8, ATK 506 (220), Thunder 400, 0%, Slots ③②①,
  Guard Lv. 2 + Convert Thunder Resistance Lv. 1. Pairs with Azure Bolt
  builds (Ful-Lagi). Decos: Guardian/Ironwall Jwl【3】, Critical Jewel II【2】,
  Critical Jewel【1】

### Best Mantles (from Zoh-Gore section)

- **Corrupted Mantle** — free damage boost since Lance attacks constantly;
  HP drains if you stop attacking
- **Mending Mantle** — passive healing in intervals after taking damage

### Best Secondary Weapon

- Any other Artian weapon except Dual Blades or Bow (both want Stamina
  Surge). Game8 suggests Charge Blade or Sword and Shield; Gunlance for a
  raw option

### Playstyle Notes (from the Zoh-Gore section; applies to Lance generally)

- Open with a Sneak Attack, then keep poke combos short enough to block —
  every combo is an animation commitment
- Time blocks for Perfect Block → triggers Offensive Guard (huge raw boost,
  realistically 100% uptime)
- After a perfect block, do not counter immediately — check if the monster
  is rearing a follow-up attack and block that too
- Holding a normal guard is fine when unsure of timing; it will not trigger
  Offensive Guard but lessens damage
- Reposition with Guard Dashes instead of sheathing

### Best Skills (Game8 star ratings)

- Damage ★★★: Offensive Guard, Weakness Exploit, Agitator, Critical Boost.
  ★★: Antivirus, Maximum Might, Resentment, Burst, Element Attack, Critical
  Status. ★: Slugger
- Comfort ★★★: Constitution, Master's Touch, Handicraft, Razor Sharp, Guard.
  ★★: Guard Up, Evade Extender, Evade Window. ★: Quick Sheathe, Speed
  Sharpening
