## Doc Thesis

- "Artian lances are miles ahead of any other lance. Even a bad one is
  probably better than all but maybe one or two." Non-Artian pick: Gore
  (Fieberschild), accepting much less damage vs T3 Tempered monsters.
- Sets optimize damage for the "average" player, not speedruns. Element and
  Blast are within 5% of each other, matchup-dependent.
- Lance is about aggression and uptime: "being aggressive and staying on
  the monster, getting as much uptime as possible."

## Best Combos

- Lazy answer / bread-and-butter: Mid Thrust x3 → Triple Poke ("MMM TT").
- Side-hopping the first two Mid Thrusts is a ~1% DPS increase.
- Optimal combo is situational: e.g. on a pure raw Blast weapon, High
  Thrust → Wide Sweep → High Thrust → Charged Counter (no parry) beats
  MMM TT by ~6% — but only if zero repositioning time is spent. With ~0.2s
  of repositioning per combo, MMM TT wins. "Or just Y spam."

## Guard / Guard Up Philosophy (feeds weapon tips)

- Guard and Guard Up are **weapon** skills in Wilds, which limits access —
  running them costs damage skills on the weapon.
- Thanks to Perfect Guard and Power Guard, both are "fairly unneeded" while
  progressing; attacks that demand Guard/Guard Up are mostly endgame
  monsters.
- Offensive Guard is the "wonder skill": 5/10/15% damage to both Raw AND
  Element, with extremely high uptime. You do NOT need a Perfect Guard to
  trigger Offensive Guard, but a Perfect Guard ALWAYS triggers it.
- "Guard and Guard Up are not going to make you do more damage, but if they
  make you comfortable, you should use them." Chip damage is covered by
  Palico healing and Perfect/Power Guard.
- Note the tension with Game8's TU4 builds, which do slot Guard Up 3 +
  Guard on Gogma weapon decos — by TU4 the weapon deco budget is roomier.

## Maximum Might on Lance

- Lance spends almost no stamina on combos and most blocks, so MM is
  sustainable; Fulgur Anjanath's Will (2pc G. Fulgur) adds a second stamina
  bar, and MM only drops once the FIRST bar starts draining.

## Progression Summary (stale — base game)

- Low Rank: armor is survival-only (Divine Blessing 2, Free Meal 2); "more
  armor = more better."
- Late LR: G. Ebony 2pc/4pc for flat attack (+3/+10), Burst 1, Udra chest.
- Early HR: LR set + Chatacabra chest bridge; make Chain Charm I for
  Burst.
- Pre-Apexes: grab G. Fulgur helm early — it is used in the endgame
  FulGore set. Pre-Gore: cap MM without decos; Agi 5.
- Weapon progression: "grab a R6 Artian as soon as Tempered monsters are
  available, use it to climb to R7, then R8." Named crafted steps (images
  9-17) NOT TRANSCRIBED — stale progression panels.

## Glossary (doc's own)

MMM TT = Mid Thrust x3 + Triple Thrust; WEX = Weakness Exploit; MM =
Maximum Might; CB = Critical Boost; Crit Ele = Critical Element; OFG =
Offensive Guard; MT = Master's Touch; RS = Razor Sharp; Coal = Coalescence;
Agi = Agitator; T1/2/3 = Tempered tiers; R1-8 = weapon rarity.
