## Core Mechanics

### Amped State and Full Release Slash

- Base Amp duration is 45 seconds; Power Prolonger 3 doubles it to
  90 seconds, resulting in more Full Release Slashes per amp window.
- The Amp Gauge is charged only in sword mode.
- Full Release Slash (FRS) is the damage loop: a ~506 MV move (up from
  ~160 in the beta). Rising Slash combo is 123 MV.
- Counterstrike procs on hyperarmor and counters — essentially a free
  +25 raw for 45 seconds at level 3, refreshed by FRS hyperarmor.
- Power Axe state grants +10 raw, only while in Axe Mode.

### Offset threshold

Offset operates on a threshold: it starts very low, and each actual
offset (topple) raises it. The shotgun-sound effect can appear without
the topple when under threshold — you can still follow up with a Heavy
Slam either way.

### Skill verdicts (doc, pre-TU4)

- Critical Boost 5: "undisputedly the best skill in the game".
- Power Prolonger: significantly improves Amp uptime.
- Rapid Morph: works but under-delivers; enables a morph playstyle for
  fun/alternative sets, "no place in the meta".
- Focus: "really, really bad" — competes with Crit Boost and Power
  Prolonger, so it doesn't compete.
- Element sets: not recommended pre-TU4 — phials became a base
  modifier, elemental cap is low, and element skills compete with
  Critical Boost.
- Antivirus gives +10% affinity (not 15% — the English description is
  wrong); frenzy gives +15% when cured.
- Gore 4pc: 2pc infects you with frenzy while fighting; 4pc grants
  +10 raw while infected, becoming 15 raw total after clearing it.
- Flayer: makes wounds appear faster; level 2 was the fastest at time
  of writing.
- Partbreaker does NOT increase Focus Strike damage (only the ~30
  damage of wound breaks).

## Other Considerations

### Food

- First slot: Meat base grants +2 raw.
- Second slot: Caprice Meal (Hi) (Airy Egg) gives +15 raw in random
  10-second intervals; Tumbler Meal (Mud and Turbid Shrimp) grants
  extra iframes on dodges; Moxie Meal (Speciality/Sild Garlic) can
  prevent one cart.
- No stand-out banger meal — eat whatever is available.

### Palico

Give the cat a Paralysis weapon or leave it at home. Para does not
interfere with Agitator. Side quests unlock revive-on-cart (half
health, once per quest), Vigor Wasps, and material stealing; cats max
at level 30.

### Mantles

Not optimized around because they do not reset between quests. The
best is Frenzy Mantle: procs extra hits on Axe Overhead/Side/Spiral
Burst Slash and Sword Overhead/Right Rising/Left Rising Slash; once
its hidden gauge is cleared it grants +30% affinity and +10% raw.

### Miscellaneous

- Power Charm grants +6 raw (obtained via the "As Gatekeeper" double
  Ajarakan arena quest in Suja).
- Self Improvement: +5 raw every 5 minutes, capped at +30 after 30.
- Meta matchup chart caption: "If it breathes, it's weak to Full
  Release Slash." (Image is a joke monster-icon grid — no per-monster
  data. NOT SHOWN ON PAGE beyond the icons.)
