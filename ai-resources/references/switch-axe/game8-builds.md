## Shared Weapon Recommendations (Game8)

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Mundus Altus

- Rarity 8, ③③③ slots. Blast or Paralysis parts auto-select Power
  Phial; Paralysis is the go-to general status, Blast for damage and
  knockdowns. Element builds use any single element (Element Phial).
- Production bonus (raw builds): Attack +5 x2, Affinity +5 x1,
  Elemental/Status +20 ("2 of 5 production bonus on Attack and the
  rest to Affinity").
- Reinforcements (raw builds): Affinity +5 x3, Attack +5 x2 — or
  Attack-heavy with one Affinity and one Sharpness +30 roll.
- Weapon decorations: Critical Jewel III【3】, Critical Jewel II【2】,
  Enhancer/Handicraft Jwl【3】 (Power Prolonger 3 + Handicraft).

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Wicked Regnum

- Rarity 8, ③③③ slots, ATK 788-823 (225-235 true raw).
- Element Focus for elemental sets, Attack Focus for raw (Attack Focus
  carries -5% Affinity — aim for at least one Affinity reinforcement
  to offset it).
- Roll targets: Set Skill Gogmapocalypse + Group Skill Lord's Soul
  (Mutual Hostility build) or Set Skill Arkveld's Hunger + Group Skill
  Lord's Soul (Arkveld Gamma build).
- Reinforce: as much Attack as possible, ideally two Attack Boost EX.
  Max roll: two Attack Boost III and one Sharpness Boost EX.

### Best Non-Artian Switch Axes

- Blazing Mahaiah (Zoh Shia) — Dragon 150, Power Phial, 5% affinity,
  ③②① slots, Whiteflame Torrent (periodic extra 50 damage procs).
- High Bolt Axe (Lagiacrus) — Thunder 300, Power Phial, innate Power
  Prolonger 2 + Convert Thunder Resistance.
- Baran Defiance (Seregios) — no element (Dragon Phial 300), 25%
  affinity, Bladescale Honing pairs with Adrenaline Rush.

### Best Mantles

- Rocksteady Mantle — keeps combos uninterrupted; tanked hits still
  trigger Counterstrike.
- Corrupted Mantle — more damage, manage the health drain.

### Best Secondary Weapons

- Any other Artian weapon except Dual Blades or Bow (both want Stamina
  Surge and elemental sets; a raw weapon like Gunlance fits better).

## Notes for Cross-Referencing (Game8)

- Build tiers per the details-page grouping: Builds 1-4 are HR 100+
  (rank `hr100`); Builds 5-11 are HR 50+.
- Dual-charm pattern: most HR50+ builds list an Appraised (RNG)
  talisman for HR100+ with a craftable HR50 alternate — record as
  "`<Charm>` (HR50+) / Appraised (HR100+)".
- The Mutual Hostility arms-piece discrepancy (summary vs details) is
  resolved in favor of the details page.
- Playstyle skills shared by nearly every build: Critical Boost,
  Power Prolonger 3 (doubles Amp duration 45 s → 90 s), Counterstrike
  (procs on hyperarmor/counter moves, +25 raw for 45 s).
