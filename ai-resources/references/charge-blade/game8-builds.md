## Shared Weapon Recommendations

### Artian Weapon (standard) — Chrono Gear

Used by every HR50+ build. Rarity 8, 810 ATK (225 true), ③③③ slots,
Impact Phial, 100 element, 5% affinity.

- Production bonus: 3x Attack +5, Elemental Attack +30 (element type:
  Paralysis — frequent free immobilization)
- Reinforcements: 4x Attack +5, 1x Sharpness +30
- Typical decorations: Guardian/Handicraft【3】, Quickswitch/Ironwall【3】,
  Magazine/Ironwall【3】(crit-focused builds swap in Critical Jewel III【3】)

### Artian Weapon (Gogma) — Promised Abyss

Used by the HR100+ elemental builds. Rarity 8, 738 ATK (205 true), ③③③,
Element Phial, 500 element, 0% affinity.

- Production bonus: Element +30, 3x Attack +5 (element type: any — craft
  per matchup)
- Focus: Element Focus · Set Skill: Gogmapocalypse · Group Skill:
  Lord's Soul (Gog Lord's Soul build; "any" for the pure elemental build)
- Reinforcements: 4x Element +50, 1x Sharpness +30
- Decorations (fire example): Blaze/Shield【3】, Enhancer/Handicraft【3】,
  Magazine/Handicraft【3】

The Arkveld Gamma Savage Axe build instead runs a raw Promised Abyss:
Attack Focus, Set Skill Gore Magala's Tyranny, 810 ATK (225), Impact
Phial, 300 element, -10% affinity; reinforcements 2x Attack +5,
Affinity +10 (EX), 2x Sharpness +30 (Affinity EX offsets Attack Focus).

### Non-Artian Alternatives

- Blazing Yirmiya — Zoh Shia tree. 792 ATK, Dragon 200, 5% affinity,
  ③②① slots, Impact Phial. Whiteflame Torrent 1 (~50 dmg proc every
  7-8s). The default non-Artian pick on most builds.
- Rebellious Charger — Seregios tree. 756 ATK, no element, 25% affinity,
  ③②①, Impact Phial. Bladescale Honing protects sharpness.
- Dear Lutemia — Lala Barina. 756 ATK, Poison 200, 15% affinity.
  Critical Status 3 + Poison Duration Up 1. (Cited on the HR36+ build.)

### Best Mantles

- Corrupted Mantle — best pick; big damage and affinity while hitting,
  health drain offset by repeated hits (use during Axe Mode).
- Rocksteady Mantle — uninterrupted Element Discharges / Savage Axe
  combos.

### Best Secondary Weapons

- Omiltika (Hunting Horn) — shares Agitator/Burst armor skills.
- Mundus Altus (Switch Axe) — benefits from the affinity-centric sets.

## Notes for Cross-Referencing

- Ten endgame builds total: 4 HR100+ (Gog Lord's Soul, Gog Magala
  Elemental, Arkveld Gamma Savage Axe, Omega Lord's Soul) and 6 HR50+.
- Dual-charm pattern on all HR50+ builds: craftable charm at HR50+,
  Appraised (RNG) charm at HR100+.
- Summary-page header typo: the Gog Lord's Soul block is titled "Gog
  Magala Elemental Charge Blade Build" on the summary page; details-page
  anchors disambiguate. Summary also lists the wrong arms piece for Gog
  Lord's Soul (see Build 1).
- The Best Skills tables rate Agitator, Offensive Guard, Artillery,
  Burst, and Flayer ★★★ for damage; Load Shells, Rapid Morph, and
  Handicraft ★★★ for comfort.
