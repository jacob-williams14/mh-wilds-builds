## Matchup Sets (transcribed briefly)

### VS. Gogmazios mission sets (HR100 milestone, §7.1)

Both use Udra Mirehelm γ / Udra Miremail γ / Lagiacrus Braces β /
Udra Mirecoil γ / Lagiacrus Greaves β + Critical Eye Lv 3 talisman.
Set/Group: Nu Udra's Mutiny, Lord's Soul, Leviathan's Fury.
Resistances: Fire 19 · Water 2 · Thunder 18 · Ice 11 · Dragon 6.

- Phase 1-2: Fire Tiltkreise (3x Fire Attack Infusion; 2x Attack +
  Sharpness reinforcement). Burst 5, WEX 5, Critical Boost 4, Razor
  Sharp 3, Fire Attack 3.
- Phase 3: Dragon Tiltkreise (3x Dragon Attack Infusion; Element Boost +
  2x Sharpness Boost) — heavily element-sided: Dragon 1417 element,
  Critical Element 3, Crit Elem/Handicraft jewels.

### VS. Omega Planetes (§3.1) / Nerscylla clone swap

NOT TRANSCRIBED (matchup-specific): Thunder Eternal Cusp set with
Mind's Eye recommended, plus a Tiltkreise swap for the Nerscylla clone
phase. See doc §3.1 for the panels and strategy.

## Notes for Cross-Referencing

- The doc's endgame philosophy is element-first: one identical armor
  shell, five element-infused Eternal Cusps. This collapses to a single
  representative build with element-swap guidance.
- Talisman meta (§2.3): generated talismans — primary Critical Eye Lv 3
  - 1 weapon slot; Counterattack Charm III remains the smithable
  fallback.
- Gogma focus (§2.1): Element Focus "wins by a landslide"; Attack and
  Affinity Focus are net DPS losses for DB.
- Doc panels supply resistance totals (Math Hunter cards), so doc-sourced
  builds do not need unverified placeholders.
