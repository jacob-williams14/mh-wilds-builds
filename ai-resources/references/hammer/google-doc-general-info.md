## Philosophy

- Preface: "Crit Meta Dead BTW. Trust Trust." — tongue-in-cheek: the
  actual framing is that heavy crit *investment* is dead. The free
  affinity sources (Weakness Exploit, 2pc Gore + Antivirus frenzy,
  Agitator) plus raw stacking do the job; Critical Boost 3 on the
  weapon is still "undisputedly the best skill in the game, once
  again."
- "The current Meta is strongly reminiscent of Iceborne's Fatalis
  Meta." Sets are ordered weakest → strongest.
- Sets assume the Power Charm (+6 raw; from the double Ajarakan arena
  quest "As Gatekeeper"), but no Might Seed / Demon Powder / drugs.
- Baseline gear requirement: access to Gog (Gogmazios). Every non-Lv3
  decoration becomes target-meldable at HR100, so you are never truly
  locked out of a set.

## Artian Crafting (§3.1)

- Materials from HR41+ Tempered Investigations: 2x Disc + 1x Tube per
  Hammer.
- Top-tier Artian base: 190 raw, 5% affinity, 20 units White
  Sharpness, always ③③③ slots. Parts of differing rarity cannot mix;
  Rarity 6/7 Artian weapons are strictly not worth it.
- Two same Element/Status parts set that element; three different
  statuses yield a pure-raw (no element) weapon; three of the same
  boosts the value.
- Each part infusion adds +5 (Attack) or +5% (Affinity).
- Reinforcing: 5 rolls, 10 Oricalcite each (50 total; trade monster
  materials/licenses at the secondary forge in Azuz). Rolls: +5%
  Affinity, +5 Element/Status, +5 Raw, +30 Sharpness.
- Recommended total rolls: 1x Sharpness, 2x Attack, 2x Element — any
  mix of Attack/Element plus Sharpness is good; dodge Affinity rolls.
- Gogma ("Gog") rolling: roll on Attack. Don't sweat Lord's Soul —
  focus on getting the main set-bonus skill your build needs.
- Dismantling refunds the Oricalcite (parts are lost).

## Food & Palico (§2.3)

- Meal slot 1: Meat base grants +2 raw. Slot 2: Caprice Meal (Hi)
  (Airy Egg) gives +15 raw in random 10-second intervals; Tumbler Meal
  (Mud/Turbid Shrimp) adds dodge i-frames; Moxie Meal (Sild Garlic)
  prevents one cart. Slot 3 depends on set and location. No stand-out
  banger meal — eat what's available.
- Palico: give it a Paralysis weapon or leave it at home (Para doesn't
  interfere with Agitator). Side quests unlock revive-on-cart, Vigor
  Wasps, and material stealing; cats cap at level 30.

## FAQ Highlights (§8)

- No Slugger/Charge Up: stun modifiers only speed up KOs, not the
  count — they can hurt by exhausting KOs early (speedrunner tools).
- No Flayer: wounds are mostly farmed on the head, which has a
  per-part cooldown, so faster wound generation barely helps.
- Mantles: "Use either Frenzy or Evasion."
- Partbreaker does not currently increase Focus Strike damage.
- Frenzy cure gives 15% affinity; Antivirus 3 gives 10% (its
  description is wrong).
- Power Charm grants +6 raw.

## Title Update History (§7)

- OBT → Release: Charge 3 and Big Bang buffed to compete with the
  Mighty Charge-centric kit.
- TU1 → TU1.5: "HUGE" — buffs across all moves; big element modifiers
  added to most final hits.
- TU1.5 → TU2: every move with "Upswing" in its name can now offset;
  Upswing Charge and Mighty Charge Upswing get 100 offset power. New
  post-offset moves: Follow-up Spin (20 MV, ticks up to 5 times) and
  Follow-up Spinslam (50 MV, then 210 MV with a 1.5 element modifier).
  Charge 3 and Mighty Charge Slam +10 MV.
- Nothing logged after TU2 (the §3/§5 sets themselves are Gogma-era).
