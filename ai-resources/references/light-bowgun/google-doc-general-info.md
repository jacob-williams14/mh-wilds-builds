## Weapon Identity

Fast, versatile weapon using superior mobility and consistent damage
to outmaneuver monsters. Chart-topping DPS with plenty of utility in
multiplayer or solo. Heavily plays around Rapid Fire (RF) Mode.

## Bowgun Mods

Two mods per gun, chosen in the Customize Bowgun menu:

| Mod | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Normal/Pierce/Spread Ammo Magazine | That ammo's capacity +1 |
| Elemental Boost Magazine | Elemental ammo capacity +1 |
| Rapid Fire Magazine | All ammo capacity +1 in RF Mode |
| Special Ammo Magazine | Special ammo capacity +1 (2 Adhesives) |
| Normal/Pierce/Spread/Elemental Ammo Powder | Ammo level +1 |
| Rapid Fire Mod | 10% faster RF gauge recovery |
| Special Ammo Mod | 10% faster special ammo recovery |

## Special Ammo

- Adhesive Ammo — sticks to the monster; ticks scale with hits that
  land on it (up to 22, preemptive detonation). Ticks deal slicing
  damage (can cut tails), can crit, respect hitzones; the final
  explosion is hitzone-independent. Generates up to ~42% RF gauge.
  Place it when gauge drops to ~50%.
- Wyvernblast — explosive at your feet; detonates up to three times,
  raw, hitzone-independent.
- 100 s recharge, capacity 1 (2 with Special Ammo Magazine).

## Rapid Fire Mode

- Gauge charges over time and from shots/chasers fired outside RF
  Mode; RF Mode is a massive damage increase — uptime is the top
  priority.
- Volley sizes: Normal 5, Pierce 3, Spread/Elemental/Status/Sticky 2.
- Spread charges gauge fastest per shot (5.0-7.0), chasers give 4.0,
  elemental shots give 0.
- Burst Step, Sidestep Reload, and RF Chasers only exist in RF Mode.

## Critical Distance

Ammo deals optimal damage only in Critical Distance (orange
crosshair). Outside it: Normal -90% far; Pierce -60% close/-90% far;
Spread -90% both; Elemental -50% raw both. Ballistics extends it.

## Playstyles (Section 6)

- Elemental — highest DPS in Wilds; standard shots in RF Mode, Burst
  Step only to reposition. Alternate shots and chasers to recharge
  gauge; consider a second identical gun for a full resource refresh.
- Normal — strongest raw ammo as of TU4, easy and comfortable; fire
  rate scales with FPS (Pierce wins below ~40 FPS).
- Pierce — the only ammo that gains DPS from chaining Burst Steps;
  extremely mobile; slightly behind Normal but more open deco slots.
- Spread — Seregios gun's Evading Reload + Bladescale Loading reward
  aggressive dodging; highest skill expression, highest recoil risk.
- Shared loop: Adhesive at ~50% gauge; build 2+ adjacent wounds and
  detonate with a charged Focus Strike for damage + gauge; Corrupted
  Mantle after/halfway through an RF gauge.

## Advanced Tips (Section 8)

- Two-gun trick: carry two identical guns — both start with full RF
  gauge and separate Adhesive charges; the stowed gun recharges.
- Burst Step through roars/attacks instead of rolling (not Spread).
- Fire a Flash Pod at your feet to instantly detonate it.
- Craft ammo during Chaser Shots (controller players).
- Sneak attacks regenerate 30% RF gauge (5-min cooldown/monster).
- As of TU4: Adhesive x2 + focus strikes + gun switching allows
  near-permanent Rapid Fire uptime.
- Focus Strikes are a real DPS gain when detonating 3+ wounds.
- Pre-buff in the hub with an Item Prolonger + Free Meal set to
  extend Mega Demondrug / Might Seed / Demon Powder durations.

## Skill Priority (endgame chapter)

- Raw weapon: Critical Boost, ammo-type Shots, Ballistics (2 pts
  mandatory for Spread), Opening Shot 3, Rapid Fire Up.
- Raw armor: Agitator 5, Maximum Might 3 (not Pierce — stamina),
  Burst 1, Latent Power/WEX, Flayer 1, Antivirus 3 (every build),
  Counterstrike 3.
- Elemental weapon: Element Attack 3 (mandatory), Critical Element 3,
  Opening Shot, Focus 3, Tetrad Shot 1, Ballistics, Rapid Fire Up.
- Elemental armor: Coalescence 3, Maximum Might 3, Burst 1,
  Agitator 5, Antivirus 3, Ambush 3 (short hunts), WEX.

## Meal / Consumables

Endgame builds assume Village food buff (+5 raw), Powercharm, Mega
Demondrug, Might Seed, Demon Powder. Spread eats Azuz Meal (Tumbler
Hi) — worth ~3 points of Evade Window.
