## Meta Build 1: Elemental Ammo (HR100+)

Strongest ammo vs every monster except non-AT Arkveld. Mutual
Hostility's one-shot protection makes it forgiving. Corrupted Mantle.

### Charms — Meta Elemental

- Craftable variant: Agitator Lv 3 talisman (Challenger Charm III)
- God-charm variant (same armor): Opening Shot Lv 2 + Coalescence Lv 2

### Weapon — Meta Elemental

Bethorned Agony (Fire example): 3x Fire Attack Infusion, Element
Focus; set Gore Magala's Tyranny, no group skill; reinforcements
2x Sharpness/Ammo Boost EX, 2x Attack Boost EX, Attack Boost III.
Decos: Salvo【3】, Crit Element/Precise【3】, Blaze/Opener【3】.
Mods: Elemental Ammo Powder + Special Ammo Magazine.
Dragon guns should be Attack Focus (1x capEX, 2x atkEX, 2x atk3).
Run two guns of the same element — gauge is per-gun (gun switching).

### Notes — Meta Elemental

Elemental Absorption activates reliably when i-framing
blight-attached attacks only if Adrenaline Rush is on the build.
Variations: Gore chest + 4 Gog (4x 1-slots + Evade Window 2, loses
Adren Rush); Sororal coil + 4 Gog (Evade Extender 2, loses 1 Peak).

Highest-DPS raw ammo in TU4; comfortable and easy. Fire rate scales

## Meta Build 4: Spread Ammo (HR100+)

Powerful but the most dangerous raw ammo (high recoil). Best in
aggressive matchups with constant Bladescale Loading procs — the
Seregios gun is too strong a skill package to forfeit even vs
Gogmartian raw. Eat Azuz Meal (Tumbler Hi) ≈ 3 extra points of
Evade Window. Corrupted or Evasion Mantle.

## Artian Crafting (doc Section 2.2)

- Forge: 1 Tube + 2 Devices, rarity 8, three parts of the same
  element, all Attack Infusion (+5 raw each).
- Reinforce (5 levels): ideal is 3x Attack Boost (+5) and 2x Capacity
  Boost (+1); Capacity is the priority. Each Affinity Boost is ~1%
  DPS loss vs Attack Boost.
- "Save-scumming": rolls are seeded at forge time — forge with junk
  parts, reinforce to preview, and reload if bad.
- Artian deco setups (ice example): meta Opener/Frost【3】+ Crit
  Elem/Frost【3】+ Focus/Frost【3】; budget Opener III + Crit Elem
  III + Frost III; gun-1 (switching) Salvo【3】+ Crit
  Element/Precise【3】+ Frost/Opener【3】.
- Artian mods: Elemental Ammo Powder + Rapid Fire Magazine.
